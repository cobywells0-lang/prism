**Traits.** One-Handed, Short, Light, Simple
**Weight.** 0.5kg
**Cost.** 1 GP

A dagger is a small and light blade, usually wielded in one hand. Its low cost and versatility make it a common choice for all walks of life.
### Dagger Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats           | Range  |
| ----- | ------------ | --------------- | ------ |
| 1     | 1            | Agi/Fin         | 5 feet |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**      | **Area**   |
| -     | -            | Weapon (dagger) | -      |

You stab or slash with a dagger. Make a melee weapon attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 1d4 damage. The damage type is your choice of Slashing or Piercing.
