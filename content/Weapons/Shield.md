**Traits.** One-Handed, Specialist
**Weight.** 4kg
**Cost.** 2 GP

A shield is a large, often round or kite-shaped piece of equipment designed to block attacks and prevent damage to the user. While holding this shield, you gain a +2 bonus to your AC. This bonus does not stack with the bonus of other Shields or Bucklers.
### Shield Basic Ability

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats           | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | --------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 2            | Agi/Phy         | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**  | **Area** |
| -        | -            | Weapon (shield) | -        |

You brace behind your shield. With two Actions you gain Damage Reduction against Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage equal to your Ability bonus until the start of your next turn or until you are not holding a shield.
