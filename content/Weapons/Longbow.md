**Traits.** Two-Handed, Ammunition (Arrow), Heavy, Specialist
**Weight.** 1kg
**Cost**. 3 GP

A Longbow is a large weapon used to fire arrows as ammunition. While they require physical strength and intensive training to use effectively, they are one of the most fearsome weapons available when put in the right hands.
### Longbow Basic Ability

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ---------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 3            | Fin/Phy          | 300 feet |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**   | **Area** |
| -        | -            | Weapon (longbow) | -        |

You loose an arrow from a longbow. Make a ranged attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 3d8 Piercing damage.
