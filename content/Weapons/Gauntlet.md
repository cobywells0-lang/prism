A gauntlet is a sturdy armoured glove, which allows punches to carry the weight needed to crush plate and scales alike.
### Gauntlet Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats             | Range  |

| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**        | **Area**   |
| -     | -            | Weapon (gauntlet) | -      |
