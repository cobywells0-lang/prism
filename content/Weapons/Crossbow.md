**Traits.** Two-Handed, Stock, Ammunition (Bolt), Reload (1A), Light, Simple
**Weight.** 3kg
**Cost.** 6 GP

A crossbow is a weapon composed of a stiff bow and a stock. While the complex construction makes it an expensive weapon to own, it's one of the easiest ranged weapons to train with and utilise.
### Crossbow Basic Ability

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats             | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 1 (reload 1) | Agi/Fin           | 150 feet |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**    | **Area** |
| -        | -            | Weapon (crossbow) | -        |

You fire a crossbow. Make a ranged attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 1d10 Piercing damage. You must reload as an Action before you can use this ability again.
