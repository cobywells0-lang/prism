**Traits.** One-Handed, Light, Simple
**Weight.** 0.5kg
**Cost.** 1 GP

A kama is a modified or repurposed agricultural scythe, popular with performers of certain martial arts for the versatility of the weapon’s unique shape.
### Kama Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats         | Range  |
| ----- | ------------ | ------------- | ------ |
| 1     | 1            | Agi/Phy       | 5 feet |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**    | **Area**   |
| -     | -            | Weapon (kama) | -      |

You slash or pick with a kama. Make a melee attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 1d6 damage. The damage type is your choice of Slashing or Piercing.
