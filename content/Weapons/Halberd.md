**Traits.** Two-Handed, Reach, Heavy, Simple
**Weight.** 3kg
**Cost.** 2 GP

A halberd is a large polearm, sporting an axe-like cutting head atop a long shaft. Favoured by armies and city guards due to the low cost relative to its effectiveness.
### Halberd Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats            | Range   |
| ----- | ------------ | ---------------- | ------- |
| 1     | 3            | Agi/Phy          | 10 feet |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**       | **Area**    |
| -     | -            | Weapon (halberd) | -       |

You cut or thrust with a halberd. Make a melee attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 3d10 damage. The damage type is your choice of Slashing or Piercing.
