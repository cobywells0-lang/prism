**Traits.** One-Handed, Simple
**Weight.** 1kg
**Cost.** 4 GP

An arming sword is a simple, short sword. It is a favourite of military groups, due to its low cost, ease to train with, and manageable weight.
### Arming Sword Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats                 | Range  |
| ----- | ------------ | --------------------- | ------ |
| 1     | 2            | Agi/Phy               | 5 feet |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**            | **Area**   |
| -     | -            | Weapon (arming sword) | -      |

You cut or thrust with an arming sword. Make a melee attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 2d8 damage. The damage type is your choice of Slashing or Piercing.
