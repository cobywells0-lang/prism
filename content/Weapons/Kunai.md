**Traits.** One-Handed, Thrown, Light, Specialist
**Weight.** 0.5kg
**Cost.** 0.5 GP

A kunai is a lightweight, cheap blade designed specifically for throwing. While difficult to use effectively, a bundle of kunai can provide a light and convenient ranged weapon option.
### Kunai Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range   |
| ----- | ------------ | -------------- | ------- |
| 1     | 1            | Agi/Fin        | 30 feet |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**     | **Area**    |
| -     | -            | Weapon (kunai) | -       |

You throw a kunai. Make an attack roll against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 1d6 Piercing damage.
