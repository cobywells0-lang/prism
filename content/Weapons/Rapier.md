**Traits.** One-Handed, Specialist
**Weight.** 1kg
**Cost.** 10 GP

A rapier is a one-handed duelling sword, characterised by a long, needle-like thrusting blade. A favourite of nobility for its complex construction and elegant style.
### Rapier Basic Ability

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats           | Range  |
| ----- | ------------ | --------------- | ------ |
| 1     | 2            | Agi/Fin         | 5 feet |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components**      | **Area**   |
| -     | -            | Weapon (rapier) | -      |

You lunge with a rapier. Make a weapon attack against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 2d6 Piercing damage.
