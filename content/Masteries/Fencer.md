**Prerequisites:** Talent: [[Parry]], [[Riposte]]

You fight elegantly and patiently, understanding that the best offense is a good defence. You gain the following benefits:

**Fencer's Basics.** You know the Talents [[Parry]] and [[Riposte]]. If you already knew both Talents when you gain this Mastery, you know one 1st-Level Talent of your choice that you are eligible to learn.

**Fighting Rhythm.** When you cause a creature to miss you with your Parry Talent, you can immediately cast Riposte as part of that same Reaction, targeting that same creature, without expending a Stamina Point.
