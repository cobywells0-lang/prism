You have grown accustomed to the dangers of adventuring. You know which wounds you can ignore, and how to quickly treat those you cannot.

**Hardiness.** Your Hit Point maximum increases by twice your level. Whenever you gain a level, your Hit Point maximum additionally increases by 2.

**Second Wind.** As an Action you can expend a number of Hit Dice up to your Proficiency bonus. For each Hit Die expended, roll it and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the number rolled plus your Physique bonus and Proficiency bonus. Once you use this Mastery in this way, you cannot use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
