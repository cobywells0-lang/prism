**Prerequisites:** Species: [[Naga]]

Your telekinetic control over your environment is much more refined. You gain the following benefits:

**Mobile Motes.** The telekinetic motes granted by your Innate Telekinesis feature can be moved up to 20 feet away from yourself, and can be moved up to 20 feet per turn, independent of your own movement.

**Grasping Force.** With 2 Actions you can telekinetically pull a creature to you. One creature of Medium size or smaller, within 5 feet of a telekinetic mote, must make a Physique saving throw (DC Instinct/Intelligence). On a failure, it is pulled up to 20 feet directly towards yourself. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
