
Your curiosity has led you to research techniques beyond what feels natural to you. Choose one class you do not have any levels in. You gain the following benefits:

**Class Basics.** You learn one Technique and one 1st-level Talent from your chosen class's Talent list.

**New Tactics.** You can choose to learn 1st-level Talents from your chosen class's Talent list, instead of your own, whenever you learn new Talents.

You can gain this Mastery multiple times, choosing a different class each time. If you later gain a level in a class chosen for this Mastery, you most replace this Mastery with another you are eligible to learn.
