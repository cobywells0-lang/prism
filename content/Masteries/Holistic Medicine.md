**Prerequisites:** Tool: [[Healing Kit]]

**Improved Healing.** When you cast a Talent that heals one or more creatures, each target regains additional Hit Points equal to your Proficiency bonus.

**Inoculation.** When you heal a creature, you can grant it Advantage on the first saving throw it makes to end or avoid the Sickened condition in the next 8 hours. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
