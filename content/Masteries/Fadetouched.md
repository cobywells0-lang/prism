**Prerequisites:** Ability: [[Fadewalk]]

You have grown familiar with the nonsensical geography of the Fade through your many visits. You gain the following benefits:

**Aberrant Navigation.** You gain a 10-foot bonus to your Speed while you are in the Fade.

**Fadecrash.** When you return from the Fade, you can choose to a space occupied by another creature. That creature must make a Physique saving throw. On a failure it takes 6d6 Anima damage. On a success it takes half damage.

If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, it dies instantly and its corpse is reduced to a fine red mist. Otherwise, you take 3d6 Anima damage and are immediately shunted to the nearest unoccupied space.
