| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**          | **Area**          |

You make a sweeping gesture, drawing a shard of stone up from the ground. Each creature within a 5-foot-radius, 15-foot-tall Cylinder centred on a point on the ground within range must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d12 Piercing damage.
