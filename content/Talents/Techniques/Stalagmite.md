**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Witch]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats                 | Range         |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------------- | ------------- |
| 0th      | 2 Actions     | Instinct/Intelligence | 30 feet       |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**          | **Area**      |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arm                   | Cube (5-foot) |

You make a sweeping gesture, drawing a shard of stone up from the ground. Each creature within a 5-foot Cube centred on a point on the ground within range must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d10 Piercing damage.
