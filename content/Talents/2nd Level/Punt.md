**Classes:** [[Brawler]], [[Shifter]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 2nd      | 2 Actions     | Physique/Agility | 5 feet   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Innate   | Instantaneous | Arms             | -        |

With two Actions you attempt to throw someone. One creature of your size or smaller within range must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it is pushed up to 30 feet. If it is pushed into another creature's space, that creature must also make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d10 Bludgeoning damage.
