**Prerequisites:** Tool: [[Poison Kit]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats                 | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | --------------------- | -------- |
| 2nd      | 1 Action     | Instinct/Intelligence | Self     |
| **Type** | **Sustain**  | **Somatics**          | **Area** |
| Tool     | 1 Minute     | Arms                  | -        |

As an Action you coat a weapon or piece of ammunition with a basic poison, which stays potent for 1 Minute. The next time that weapon or piece of ammunition is used to damage a creature, the target must make a Physique saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 3d6 Vitalic damage and has Disadvantage on all skill checks and attack rolls until the end of its next turn. On a success it takes half damage only.

**When cast at higher levels:** The Vitalic damage increases by 1d6 for every level over 2nd.
