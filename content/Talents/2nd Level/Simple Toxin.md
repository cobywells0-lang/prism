| -------- | ------------ | --------------------- | -------- |

| **Type** | **Duration** | **Somatics**          | **Area** |
| Tool     | 1 Minute     | Arms                  | -        |

**Tools:** [[Poison Kit]]

As an Action you coat a weapon or piece of ammunition with a basic poison, which stays potent for 1 Minute. The next time that weapon or piece of ammunition is used to damage a creature, the target must make a Physique saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 3d6 Vitalic damage and is Sickened until the end of its next turn. On a success it takes half damage only.

**When cast at higher levels:** The Vitalic damage increases by 1d6 for every level over 2nd.
