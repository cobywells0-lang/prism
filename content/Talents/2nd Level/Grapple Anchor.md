**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Assassin]], [[Commander]], [[Hunter]], [[Knave]], [[Swashbuckler]]

| Level    | Casting Time | Stats        | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------ | -------- |
| 2nd      | 1 Action     | -            | 30 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration** | **Somatics** | **Area** |
| Tool     | 1 Minute     | Arm          | -        |

**Tools:** [[Climbing Kit]]

As an Action, you create a floating anchor at a point you can see within range. The anchor remains in place for the duration.

While holding a Climbing Kit and within 30 feet of the anchor, you can use an Action to swing around it, moving to a space on the opposite side of the anchor, at a distance no further from it than your starting space.
