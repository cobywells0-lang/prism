**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Commander]], [[Deadeye]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Witch]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats        | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------ | -------- |
| 2nd      | 2 Actions     | -            | 10 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics** | **Area** |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arm, Voice   | -        |

With 2 Actions you call forth a familiar spirit, a magical entity loyal to its summoner. A [[Familiar]] appears at a point within range. This Familiar remains until it is destroyed, or until you cast this ability again to summon a new Familiar. Your Familiar understands the Languages you are proficient in, but cannot speak. You can communicate telepathically with it while within 120 feet, and it obeys your commands to the best of its ability. 

In combat your Familiar acts during your turn, and can move up to its Speed and use Reactions on its own. You can spend an Action to telepathically command your Familiar to take an action to move, interact with an object, or attempt a skill check.

Your familiar may take the form of a Tiny creature, such as a raven, toad or pseudodragon, or it may appear as a formless mass of energy akin to a wisp. Regardless of its appearance, it uses the [[Familiar]] stat block.
