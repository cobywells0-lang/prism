You force a creature to take a harmless concoction, but it really seems like it should do something nasty. One creature within range must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and skill checks for up to 1 Minute. When you Sustain this ability, the target repeats its saving throw. On a failure it takes 1d8 Psychic damage. On a success the ability ends.
