**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Deadeye]], [[Hunter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]]

| Level  | Casting Time  | Stats            | Range            |
| ------ | ------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| 2nd    | 3 Actions     | Agility/Instinct | 120 feet         |
| Type   | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area**         |
| Weapon | Instantaneous | Arms             | Sphere (10-Foot) |

**Weapons:** [[Crossbow]], [[Longbow]]

With 3 Actions you rapidly fire a volley of ammunition that arcs over obstacles. Expend 5 pieces of ammunition. Each creature within a 10-foot Sphere centred on a point within range must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 3d8 Piercing damage. On a success, it takes half damage.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d8 for every level above 2nd.
