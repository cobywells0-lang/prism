**Classes:** [[Brawler]], [[Knave]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats           | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------- | ------------------------ |
| 2nd      | 1 Actions     | Finesse/Agility | Self                     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**    | **Area**                 |
| Innate   | Instantaneous | Arm             | Cone Emanation (10-foot) |

With two Actions you toss debris into the eyes of your foes. Each creature within a 10-foot Cone Emanation must make an Instinct saving throw. On a failure, it is [[Blind]] until the start of your next turn.
