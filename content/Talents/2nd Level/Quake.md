**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Brawler]], [[Gambler]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats         | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| 2nd      | 1 Action      | Physique/Soul | Self                     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**  | **Area**                 |
| Innate   | Instantaneous | Arms          | Cone Emanation (15-foot) |

As an Action you slam the ground, propelling a shockwave through the earth. Each creature within a 20-foot Cone Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage and falls Prone. On a success, it takes half damage only.

**When cast at higher levels:** The Bludgeoning damage increases by 1d6 for every level over 2nd.
