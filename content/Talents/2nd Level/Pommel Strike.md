**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Buckler]], [[Crossbow]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Kama]], [[Rapier]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 2nd      | 1 Action      | Physique/Agility | 5 Feet   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm              | -        |

With an Action you strike at the head with the blunt end of your weapon. One creature within 5 feet must make a Physique saving throw. On a failure, the target takes 1d10 Bludgeoning damage and is [[Dazed]] 2 until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage only.

**When cast at higher levels:** The Bludgeoning damage increases by 1d10 for every level over 2nd.
