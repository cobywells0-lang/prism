**Classes:** [[Knave]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Witch]]

| Level    | Casting Time          | Stats         | Range               |
| -------- | --------------------- | ------------- | ------------------- |
| 2nd      | 2 Actions             | Soul/Instinct | 10 feet             |
| **Type** | **Duration**          | **Somatics**  | **Area**            |
| Magic    | 1 Minute (Sustain 2A) | Arms, Voice   | True Cube (15-foot) |

With two Actions you call on elemental lightning. Choose a point you can see within range. Each creature within a 15-foot True Cube centred on that point must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 3d8 Lightning damage and is [[Deaf]] until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage only.

When you Sustain this Talent, the Cube moves 15 feet directly away from the point you were when this Talent was cast. Creatures within the Cube then repeat the saving throw.

**When cast at higher levels:** The Lightning damage increases by 1d8 for every level over 2nd.
