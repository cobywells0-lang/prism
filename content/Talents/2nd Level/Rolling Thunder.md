**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Gambler]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Witch]]

| Level | Cast Actions  | Stats          | Range          |
| ----- | ------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| 2     | 3             | Soul/Instinct  | 10 feet        |
|       | **Sustain**   | **Components** | **Area**       |
|       | 1 Minute (2A) | Magic          | Cube (15-foot) |

With three Actions you call on elemental lightning. Choose a point you can see within range. Each creature within a 15-foot Cube centred on that point must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d10 Lightning damage and is [[Deaf]] until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage only.

When you Sustain this Talent, the Cube moves 15 feet directly away from the point you were when this Talent was cast. Creatures within the Cube then repeat the saving throw.
