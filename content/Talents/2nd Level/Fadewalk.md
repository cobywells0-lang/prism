**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Assassin]], [[Commander]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats        | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------ | -------- |
| 2nd      | 1 Action     | -            | Self     |
| **Type** | **Duration** | **Somatics** | **Area** |
| Magic    | 1 Round      | Legs, Voice  | -        |

As an Action you step into [[the Fade]], a demiplane tangential to your plane of origin. After arriving in the Fade, you can move up to your Speed.

You reappear on your original plane at the start of your next turn, unless you choose to return sooner (no action required). If your space is occupied, you appear in the nearest unoccupied space.
