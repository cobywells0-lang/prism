**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Artisan]], [[Hunter]]

| Level | Casting Time | Stats                 | Range   |
| ----- | ------------ | --------------------- | ------- |
| 2nd   | 2 Actions    | Instinct/Intelligence | 60 feet |
| **Type**  | **Duration**     | **Somatics**              | **Area**    |
| Magic | 1 Minute     | Arm, Voice            | -       |

With 2 Actions you create a 5-foot cube of acidic, transparent green slime in an unoccupied point you can see within range. The slime remains in that space for the duration and blocks movement through its space.

Upon creation, each creature within 5 feet of the cube must make a Physique saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d4 Acid damage and is pushed 10 feet directly away from the cube. A creature repeats this saving throw the first time it moves to a space within 5 feet of the cube on each of its turns.

The cube has an AC of 14 and is Immune to all damage types except Fire. If it takes any Fire damage it explodes, destroying it, and each creature within 10 feet of it must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d8 Fire damage. On a success, it takes half damage only.
