**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Artisan]], [[Brawler]], [[Gambler]], [[Magus]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats         | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------- | -------- |
| 2nd      | Reaction      | Physique/Soul | Self     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**  | **Area** |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arm           | -        |

As a Reaction when you would take damage, you surround yourself in protective ice. You gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to 20 plus your Talent bonus, which take as much of the triggering damage as possible. At the start of each of your turns, you can choose to either lose 10 of your remaining Temporary Hit Points, or all of them. While any of these Temporary Hit Points remain, you are [[Frozen]].

**When cast at higher levels:** The number of Temporary Hit Points granted increases by 10 for every level over 2nd.
