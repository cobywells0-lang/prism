| 2        | Reaction     | Physique/Soul | Self     |

| Magic    | -            | Arm           | -        |

As a Reaction when you would take damage, you surround yourself in protective ice. You gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to 10 times your Talent bonus, which take as much of the triggering damage as possible. At the start of each of your turns, you can choose to either lose 15 remaining Temporary Hit Points, or all of them. While any of these Temporary Hit Points remain, you are [[Frozen]].
