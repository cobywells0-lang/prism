**Classes:** [[Assassin]], [[Deadeye]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats            | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| 2nd      | 2 Actions     | Finesse/Instinct | -                        |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area**                 |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arms             | Cone Emanation (60-foot) |

**Weapons:** [[Crossbow]], [[Kunai]], [[Longbow]]

With 2 Actions you loose a trio of projectiles. Make a ranged weapon attack against up to three different creatures within a 60-foot Cone Emanation. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Weapon damage.

**When cast at higher levels:** The maximum number of different creatures you can make an attack against increases by 1 for every level over 2nd.
