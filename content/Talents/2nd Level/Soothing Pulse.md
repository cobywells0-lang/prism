| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats             | Range                      |
| -------- | ------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| 2nd      | 2 Actions     | Soul/Intelligence | Self                       |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**      | **Area**                   |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arm, Voice        | Sphere Emanation (10-foot) |

With two Actions you emit a burst of healing energy. Each Ally within a 10-foot Sphere Emanation regains a number of Hit Points equal to 2d6 plus your Talent bonus.

**When cast at higher levels:** Healing increases by 1d6 for every level over 2nd.
