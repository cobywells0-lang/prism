**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Commander]], [[Gambler]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats         | Range           |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| 0th      | 2 Actions     | Soul/Instinct | 60 feet         |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**  | **Area**        |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Voice         | Sphere (5-foot) |

You call out and bring celestial flame upon your foes. Target a point you can see within range. Each creature within a 5-foot Sphere centred on that point must make an Agility saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 1d6 Radiant damage. On a successful save, it takes half damage.
