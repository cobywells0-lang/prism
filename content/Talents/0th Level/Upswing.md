**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Halberd]], [[Kama]], [[Meteor Hammer]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 0th      | 2 Actions     | Physique/Agility | Weapon   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm              | -        |

With 2 Actions you strike with an upwards motion. Make a melee weapon attack against one creature within reach. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Weapon damage and is pushed 5 feet directly upwards.
