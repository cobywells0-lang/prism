| 0th      | 1 Action     | Finesse/Instinct | 60 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration** | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Magic    | 1 Minute     | Arms             | -        |

As an Action you form six razor-sharp shards of ice, which hover over your shoulders for the duration. When the Talent ends, any remaining shards are destroyed.

As an Action you can propel a shard at a creature within range. Make a ranged weapon attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Frost damage and is Slow 1 until the end of its next turn. Hit or miss, the shard is destroyed.

A shard created by this Talent counts as a Kunai for the purposes of Talent prerequisites. A shard used in casting a Talent is destroyed.
