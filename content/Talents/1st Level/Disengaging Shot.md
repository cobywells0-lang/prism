**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Assassin]], [[Deadeye]], [[Gambler]], [[Hunter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats           | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 2 Actions     | Finesse/Agility | 60 Feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**    | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm, Legs       | -        |

**Weapons:** [[Crossbow]], [[Kunai]], [[Longbow]]

You cover your movements with weapon fire. Make a ranged weapon attack against one creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Weapon damage and you can jump up to 20 feet in any direction. This movement does not trigger Reactions.

**When cast at higher levels:** Jump range increases by 10 feet for every level over 1st.
