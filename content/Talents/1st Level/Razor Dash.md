**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Assassin]], [[Deadeye]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Warrior]], Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Dagger]], [[Kama]], [[Kunai]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats           | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 1 Action      | Finesse/Agility | Self     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**    | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Legs, Arm       | -        |

As an Action you move up to 30 feet in a straight line. This movement can pass through the spaces of other creatures. Each creature you move through must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d6 Slashing damage.

**When cast at higher levels:** Movement increases by 10 feet for every level over 1st.
