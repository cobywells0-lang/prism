**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Commander]], [[Hunter]], [[Witch]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats        | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------ | -------- |
| 1st      | 3 Actions    | -            | 5 feet   |
| **Type** | **Sustain**  | **Somatics** | **Area** |
| Magic    | 1 Minute     | Voice        | -        |

With three Actions you summon the avatar of an Archfey - a translucent image that might resemble a woodland creature, pixie, or wisp - at a point within range. You may ask this avatar one question, which it will answer truthfully, to the best of its knowledge, before disappearing.

While this answer cannot contain a deliberate falsehood, the truth may be presented in such a way as to be intentionally misleading, opaque, cryptic, or otherwise unhelpful.
