**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Crossbow]], [[Kunai]], [[Longbow]]

| Level        | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range                    |
| ------------ | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1st          | 2 Actions     | Finesse/Instinct | Self                     |
| **Type**     | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area**                 |
| Magic Weapon | Instantaneous | Arm              | Line Emanation (60-foot) |

With 2 Actions you propel a piercing projectile with spectacular force. Each creature within a 60-foot Line Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 2d6 Piercing damage and is [[Bleeding]] 1. On a success it takes half damage only. An affected creature makes a Physique saving throw at the end of each of its turns, losing the [[Bleeding]] condition on a success.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d6 for every level over 1st.
