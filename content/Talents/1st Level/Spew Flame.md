**Classes:** [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats         | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1st      | 2 Actions     | Soul/Instinct | Self                     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**  | **Area**                 |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arms          | Line Emanation (20-foot) |

With 2 Actions you produce a stream of flames from your palms. Each creature within a 20-foot Line Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d6 Fire damage. On a success, it takes half that damage.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d6 for every level above 1st.
