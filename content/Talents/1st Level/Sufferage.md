**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Assassin]], [[Deadeye]], [[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats        | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------ | ------------------------ |
| 1st      | 2 Actions     | Finesse/Soul | Self                     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics** | **Area**                 |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arms         | Cone Emanation (15-foot) |

You cut your hand with a Slashing or Piercing weapon and with your blood, you sap the life of others. You take 1d6 Weapon damage, which cannot be reduced. Each creature within a 15-foot Cone Emanation must make a Physique saving throw. On a failure it takes 2d8 Vitalic damage. On a success, it takes half that damage.
