**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Shifter]]

| Level  | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range                    |
| ------ | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1st    | 2 Actions     | Agility/Instinct | Self                     |
| **Type**   | **Duration**      | **Somatics**         | **Area**                     |
| Innate | Instantaneous | Legs, Arms       | Cone Emanation (15-foot) |

With 2 Actions you jump up to twice your Speed. This movement does not trigger Reactions. You can then expend 1 Primal Energy point to slash at your foes. Each creature within a 15-foot Cone Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure it takes 1d8 Slashing damage and is Bleeding 1 until the end of its next turn.
