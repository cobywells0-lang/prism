**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Commander]], [[Magus]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions            | Stats             | Range    |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 1 Action                | Soul/Intelligence | 30 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration**            | **Somatics**      | **Area** |
| Magic    | 10 Minutes (1A Sustain) | Arm, Voice        | -        |

As an Action you draw a short earthen pillar up out of the ground at an unoccupied point within range. The pillar is a 5-foot cube, and can provide [[Cover|Partial Cover]] to Medium or smaller creatures. It has a number of Hit Points equal to your Talent bonus, is Immune to Corruption, Psychic and Vitalic damage, and has Damage Reduction against all other damage types equal to your Talent bonus.

If the Talent is Sustained for its full duration, the pillar remains until destroyed. Otherwise, it crumbles into loose dirt when the Talent is ended.

**When cast at higher levels:** You create two additional pillars for each level above 1st. Adjacent pillars form a continuous wall, and pillars can be formed on top of other pillars created by this Talent. A wall created by multiple connected pillars may provide greater cover, or cover for creatures larger than Medium, at the GM's discretion.
