**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Assassin]], [[Deadeye]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Swashbuckler]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats        | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------ | -------- |
| 1st      | 1 Action      | -            | Self     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics** | **Area** |
| Innate   | Instantaneous | Legs         | -        |

As an Action you roll defensively. Move up to your Speed. This movement doesn't trigger Reactions, and can move through the space of other creatures. You have [[Cover|Partial Cover]] against the first attack or area that targets you before the start of your next turn.
