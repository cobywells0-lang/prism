| 1st      | 1 Action      | Finesse/Physique | Weapon   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm              | -        |

**Weapons:** [[Arming Sword]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Halberd]], [[Kama]], [[Meteor Hammer]], [[Rapier]]

As an Action you lunge forward to start a vicious combo. Move up to half your Speed and make a melee weapon attack against one creature within reach. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Weapon damage and the next Weapon Talent you cast on this turn costs one fewer Actions to cast (minimum 1).

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d8 for every level above 1st.
