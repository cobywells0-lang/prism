**Classes:** [[Assassin]], [[Brawler]], [[Deadeye]], [[Hunter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats           | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | Reaction      | Finesse/Agility | 5 Feet   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**    | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm             | -        |

**Weapons:** [[Arming Sword]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Kama]], [[Rapier]]

You capitalise on a failed attack. As a Reaction when a creature you can see misses you with a melee attack, make a melee weapon attack against that creature. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Weapon damage and has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d8 for every level over 1st.
