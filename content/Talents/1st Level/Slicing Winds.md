**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Artisan]], [[Assassin]], [[Brawler]], [[Deadeye]], [[Gambler]], [[Magus]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level        | Cast Actions | Stats            | Range    |
| ------------ | ------------ | ---------------- | -------- |
| 1st          | 1 Action     | Finesse/Physique | 90 feet  |
| **Type**     | **Sustain**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Magic Weapon | 1 Minute     | Arm              | -        |

As an Action you imbue a melee weapon with four arcane motes of elemental air. When you cast this ability, and as an Action for the duration, you can expend one mote to make a Ranged Magic attack against one creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Thunder damage. This ability ends after 1 Minute, or when all four motes are expended.
