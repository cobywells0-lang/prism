| 1st      | Reaction      | Finesse/Instinct | Self     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |

**Weapons:** [[Arming Sword]], [[Buckler]], [[Dagger]], [[Kama]], [[Rapier]], [[Shield]]

You disrupt an attack with a defensive flourish. As a Reaction when a creature you can see hits you with a melee attack, you force it to make a Finesse saving throw. On a failure, the attack misses instead.
