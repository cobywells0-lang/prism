**Classes:** [[Artisan]], [[Magus]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]]

| Level | Casting Time  | Stats                 | Range                      |
| ----- | ------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1st   | 1 Action      | Instinct/Intelligence | Self                       |
| Type  | Duration      | Somatics              | Area                       |
| Magic | Instantaneous | Arm                   | Sphere Emanation (10-foot) |

As an Action you produce a cloud of acidic vapours around you. Each creature within a 10-foot Sphere Emanation must make a Physique saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d6 Acid damage and has Disadvantage on the first weapon attack it makes on its next turn.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d6 for every level above 1st.
