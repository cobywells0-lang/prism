**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Crossbow]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Halberd]], [[Kama]], [[Kunai]], [[Longbow]], [[Meteor Hammer]], [[Rapier]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 2 Actions     | Finesse/Physique | Weapon   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm              | -        |

With 2 Actions you attempt to knock an item out of your foe’s grasp. One creature within range must make a Finesse saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d4 Weapon damage and drops one item of your choice. On a success, it takes half damage only.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d4 for every level over 1st.
