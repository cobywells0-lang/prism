**Classes:** [[Brawler]], [[Commander]], [[Shifter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Casting Time | Stats         | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 1 Action     | Physique/Soul | 30 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration** | **Somatics**  | **Area** |
| Innate   | 1 Minute     | Voice         | -        |

As an Action you call out a foe, goading it to engage with you. One creature within Range that can hear you must make an Instinct saving throw. On a failure, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than yourself and cannot willingly move away from you for the duration. An affected creature repeats this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the Talent on a success. The Talent also ends if the affected creature is ever outside of Range, or if another creature Taunts it.

**When cast at higher levels:** You can target one additional creature for every level above 1st.
