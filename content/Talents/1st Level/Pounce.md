**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Assassin]], [[Brawler]], [[Hunter]], [[Shifter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 1 Action      | Agility/Instinct | 30 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Innate   | Instantaneous | Legs             | -        |

You leap upon unsuspecting prey. Jump to a point within range. If you jump into the same space as another creature, that creature must make a Physique saving throw. On a failure, the target falls Prone and is pushed 5 feet to an adjacent space. On a success, you are instead shunted to the nearest empty space.

**When cast at higher levels:** Range increases by 10 feet for every level over 1st.
