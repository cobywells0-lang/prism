**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Deadeye]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Witch]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | 1 Action      | Agility/Instinct | 120 feet |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Arm              | -        |

As an Action you form a spear of frost and hurl it. Make a magic ranged attack against a creature within range. On a hit, it takes 2d6 Frost damage, and the spear remains lodged in the target until the start of your next turn. If the target willingly moves before then, it takes an additional 2d6 Frost damage as the spear shatters.

**When cast at higher levels:** Both instances of damage increase by 1d6 for every level over 1st.
