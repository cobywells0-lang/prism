**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Halberd]], [[Meteor Hammer]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats            | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1st      | 2 Actions     | Physique/Agility | Self                     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area**                 |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arms             | Cone Emanation (15-foot) |

With two Actions you swing wildly at a group of foes. Each creature within a 15-foot Cone Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d6 Weapon damage. On a success it takes half damage.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d6 for every level over 1st.
