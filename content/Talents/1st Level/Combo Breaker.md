| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 1st      | Reaction      | Physique/Agility | 5 Feet   |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Weapon   | Instantaneous | Arm              | -        |

**Weapons:** [[Arming Sword]], [[Buckler]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Halberd]], [[Meteor Hammer]], [[Shield]]

You finish a ruthless string of attacks with a crippling blow. As a Reaction after hitting a target with two consecutive attacks, make a melee weapon attack against that creature. On a hit the target takes 3d6 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed 20 feet directly away from you.

**When cast at higher levels:** Push increases by 5 feet for every level over 1st.
