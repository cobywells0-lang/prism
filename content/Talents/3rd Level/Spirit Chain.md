**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Commander]], [[Knave]], [[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Casting Time          | Stats             | Range            |
| -------- | --------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| 3rd      | 1 Action              | Soul/Intelligence | 30 feet          |
| **Type** | **Duration**          | **Somatics**      | **Area**         |
| Magic    | 1 Minute (Sustain 1A) | Arm               | Sphere (10-foot) |

As an Action you reach out and attempt to bind a foe's spirit. One creature within range must make a Soul saving throw. On a failure it is Cursed for up to 1 Minute. While Cursed in this way, it cannot move outside of a 10-foot Sphere centred on the space it was originally Cursed. Each time you Sustain this ability, the target repeats the saving throw, losing the Cursed condition on a success.
