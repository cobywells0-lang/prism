**Prerequisite:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Commander]], [[Gambler]], [[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]]

| Level | Cast Actions  | Stats             | Range           |
| ----- | ------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| 3     | 2             | Soul/Intelligence | 30 feet         |
|       | **Sustain**   | **Components**    | **Area**        |
|       | 1 Minute (1A) | Magic             | Sphere (5-foot) |

With 2 Actions you reach out and attempt to bind a foe's spirit. One creature within range must make a Soul saving throw. On a failure it is Cursed for up to 1 Minute. While Cursed in this way, it cannot move outside of a 5-foot Sphere centred on the space it was originally Cursed. Each time you Sustain this ability, the target repeats the saving throw, losing the Cursed condition on a success.
