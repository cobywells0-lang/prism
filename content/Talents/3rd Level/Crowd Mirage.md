**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Assassin]], [[Deadeye]], [[Magus]], [[Warrior]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats        | Range            |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| 3rd      | 2 Actions     | -            | Self             |
| **Type** | **Sustain**   | **Somatics** | **Area**         |
| Magic    | 1 Minute (1A) | Arms         | Sphere (15-foot) |

With 2 Actions you construct a crowd of illusory clones within a 15-foot Sphere centred on yourself. While within this Sphere you are Concealed. When you deal damage to a creature, you are no longer Concealed from that creature by this Talent until you next Sustain it.

Each time you Sustain this Talent, you can cause the clones to either act randomly, or mimic your movements and speech in unison, until you next Sustain it.
