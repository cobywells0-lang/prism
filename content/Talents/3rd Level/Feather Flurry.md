**Classes** [[Commander]], [[Hunter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Witch]]

| Level    | Casting Time          | Stats         | Range                      |
| -------- | --------------------- | ------------- | -------------------------- |
| 3rd      | 2 Actions             | Soul/Instinct | Self                       |
| **Type** | **Duration**          | **Somatics**  | **Area**                   |
| Magic    | 1 Minute (Sustain 1A) | Voice         | Sphere Emanation (10-foot) |

As an Action you call upon a flock of birds, bats, pixies, or some other winged creatures to aid you. For the duration, the creatures swarm harmlessly in a 5-foot Sphere Emanation. You and all Allies within this area have [[Cover|Partial Cover]] from attacks and area effects originating from outside of the area. All creatures have Disadvantage on Perception checks that involve seeing or hearing something within the area.
