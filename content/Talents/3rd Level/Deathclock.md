| ------------ | --------------------- | --------------- | -------- |
| 3rd          | 3 Actions             | Finesse/Agility | 60 feet  |
| **Type**     | **Duration**          | **Somatics**    | **Area** |
| Magic Weapon | 1 Minute (Sustain 1A) | Arm, Voice      | -        |

**Weapons:** [[Arming Sword]], [[Dagger]], [[Halberd]], [[Kama]]

With 3 Actions, you teleport behind one creature within range, and slash at its spirit. The target must make a Soul saving throw. On a failure, it is Cursed. On a success, you instead take 1d6 Psychic damage as your one-liner falls flat.

While Cursed in this way, the target repeats the saving throw each time you Sustain this ability. On a success, or when the ability ends, the target takes 6d6 Corruption damage, plus an additional 2d6 Corruption damage for each saving throw it failed against this ability.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d6 for every level above 3rd.
