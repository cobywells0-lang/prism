**Abilities:** [[Familiar Spirit]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats             | Range          |
| -------- | ------------- | ----------------- | -------------- |
| 3rd      | 2 Actions     | Soul/Intelligence | -              |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**      | **Area**       |
| Magic    | Instantaneous | Voice             | Line (30-foot) |

With 2 Actions while you can communicate telepathically with your Familiar, you weaponize its arcane form by having it propel through your foes. Each creature within a 30-foot Line originating from your Familiar must make a Soul saving throw. On a failure it takes 3d10 Corruption damage. If only one creature fails this saving throw, it takes an additional 1d10 Corruption damage and is [[Slow]] 2 until the end of its next turn. Your Familiar is then shunted to the space at the far end of the line.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d10 for every level above 3rd.
