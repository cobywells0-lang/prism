**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Brawler]], [[Shifter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Casting Time  | Stats            | Range    |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| 3rd      | 2 Actions     | Physique/Agility | Self     |
| **Type** | **Duration**  | **Somatics**     | **Area** |
| Innate   | Instantaneous | Legs             | -        |

With 2 Actions you charge into your foes, barging and trampling as you go. Move in a straight line up to 30 feet. Each creature whose space you move through must make a Physique saving throw. On a failed save the target takes 2d10 Bludgeoning damage and falls [[Prone]]. On a successful save it takes half damage only.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d10 for every level above 3rd.
