**Classes:** [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Assassin]], [[Hunter]], [[Shifter]], [[Warrior]]

| Level | Casting Time | Stats            | Range   |
| ----- | ------------ | ---------------- | ------- |
| 3rd   | 1 Action     | Agility/Instinct | 60 feet |
| **Type**  | **Duration**     | **Somatics**         | **Area**    |
| Tool  | 1 Minute     | Arm              | -       |

**Tools:** [[Trapping Kit]]

As an Action you cast out a bola - a thrown weapon designed to entangle limbs. Make a ranged weapon attack against one creature within range. On a hit, two of the target's legs, arms, or wings are Restrained for the duration. The target makes a Physique saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a success, it is no longer Restrained. On a failure, it takes 1d6 Slashing damage.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d6 for every level above 3rd.
