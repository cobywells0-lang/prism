**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Shifter]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]], Species: [[Orc]]

| Level    | Cast Actions            | Stats             | Range    |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- | -------- |
| 3rd      | Reaction                | Physique/Instinct | 30 feet  |
| **Type** | **Duration**            | **Somatics**      | **Area** |
| Magic    | 10 Minutes (1A Sustain) | Voice             | -        |

As a Reaction when a Medium creature within range dies, you invest a crude mote of undeath into the fresh corpse. That corpse becomes a [[Wight]]. It takes its turn immediately after yours, during which it will mindlessly chase and attack the closest creature it can see, other than you. The Wight drops to zero Hit Points when this Talent ends.
