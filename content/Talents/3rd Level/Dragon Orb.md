**Classes:** [[Sorcerer]]

| Level    | Casting Time          | Stats         | Range            |
| -------- | --------------------- | ------------- | ---------------- |
| 3rd      | 2 Actions             | Soul/Instinct | 120 feet         |
| **Type** | **Duration**          | **Somatics**  | **Area**         |
| Magic    | 1 Minute (Sustain 1A) | Arms, Voice   | Sphere (20-foot) |

With 2 Actions you begin forming a sphere of crackling energy between your palms. This talent can be sustained multiple times per turn, and on the same turn it is cast. If you perform any action other than sustaining it, the talent fizzles out and ends early. Each time you sustain this talent, the sphere grows larger and more vibrant.

Immediately after you cast or sustain this talent, you can choose to release the sphere and launch it to a point you can see within range. Each creature within a 20-foot Sphere centred on that point must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 3d8 Lightning damage. This damage is increased by 1d8 for each time you Sustained the talent before releasing it.

**When cast at higher levels:** Damage increases by 1d8 for every level above 3rd.
