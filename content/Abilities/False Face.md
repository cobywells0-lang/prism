**Prerequisites:** Tool: Disguise Kit

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 3            | Fin/Int        | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Tool           | -        |

With 3 Actions you don a convincing disguise. You change your appearance to that of a generic individual, or a specific person you have seen within 30 feet of yourself. You can change your perceived species, gender and clothing, but cannot change your size or general body plan. Your capabilities do not change, only your appearance. This disguise lasts until you finish a Short or Long Rest, or until you doff it as an Action.

A creature can attempt to see through your disguise with an Insight check against your ability DC. On a success, it is aware that you are disguised, but cannot see your true form.
