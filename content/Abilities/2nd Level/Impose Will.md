**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Artisan]], [[Commander]], [[Gambler]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats             | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------------- | -------- |
| 2        | 2            | Soul/Intelligence | 30 feet  |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**    | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Magic             | -        |

You call out a demand, the word laced with arcane influence. One creature within range must make an Instinct saving throw. On a failed save, the target performs the action associated with the command word you choose on its next turn. After performing the chosen action, the target then ends its turn. If the target cannot hear you, or does not share a language proficiency with you, it automatically succeeds this saving throw.

| Command Word | Action                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Approach     | The target moves towards you until it is within 5 feet, until its path is blocked by an obstacle or an obvious hazard, or until it runs out of movement. |
| Fall         | The target falls Prone.                                                                                                                                  |
| Drop         | The target drops one held item of your choice at its feet.                                                                                               |
| Utilize      | The target moves towards one object of your choice and utilizes it (such as pulling a lever, or opening a door).                                         |
