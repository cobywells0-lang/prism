With 2 Actions you call forth a familiar spirit, a magical entity loyal to its summoner. A Familiar appears at a point within range. This Familiar remains until it is destroyed, or until you cast this ability again to summon a new Familiar. Your Familiar understands the Languages you are proficient in, but cannot speak. You can communicate telepathically with it while within 120 feet, and it obeys your commands to the best of its ability. 

As an Action while you can telepathically communicate with your Familiar, you can expend a use of this ability to connect your minds. For 1 Minute you can see through your familiar's eyes, hear through its ears, and feel everything it can feel. Additionally, you can speak through your familiar's mouth, using your own voice. For the duration you are unable to sense or act through your own body, you are Blind, Deaf and Incapacitated. You can end this effect early (no action required).

In combat your Familiar acts during your turn, and can move up to its Speed and use Reactions on its own. You can spend an Action to telepathically command your Familiar to take an action to move, interact with an object, or attempt a skill check.

Your familiar may take the form of a Tiny creature, such as a raven, toad or pseudodragon, or it may appear as a formless mass of energy akin to a wisp. Regardless of its appearance, it has the stat block listed below.
