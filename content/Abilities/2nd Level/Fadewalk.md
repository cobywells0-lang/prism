As an Action you step in a direction most can't sense, and arrive in the Fade. While in the Fade you are Incorporeal and Hidden from creatures not in the Fade of your original plane, and you see your original plane as hazy, vague shapes cast in shades of grey. You can move, and can see and interact with other creatures and objects in the Fade. You reappear on your original plane at the end of your turn, unless you choose to return sooner (no action required). If your space is occupied, you appear in the nearest unoccupied space.
