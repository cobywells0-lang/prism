| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats           | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | --------------- | -------- |
| 2        | 2            | Finesse/Agility | 30 Feet  |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**  | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Weapon*         | -        |

*Any Ranged weapon you have Proficiency with.

You cover your movements with weapon fire. Make a ranged weapon attack against one creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Weapon damage and you can jump up to 20 feet in any direction. This movement does not trigger Reactions.
