**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Brawler]], [[Gambler]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| 2        | 2            | Physique/Soul  | Self                     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area**                 |
| 4/LR     | -            | Innate         | Cone Emanation (15-foot) |

With 2 Actions you propel a shockwave through the earth. Each creature within a 15-foot Cone Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and falls Prone. On a success, it takes half damage only.
