As a Reaction when you are hit with a Melee attack, you defend yourself with sharp bristles. The triggering creature must make a Finesse saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d8 Piercing damage and the damage you take from the triggering attack is reduced by that same amount. On a success, it takes half damage only.

With 2 Actions you can expend a Primal Energy point to propel a pair of spines at nearby foes. Make a Ranged attack against two different creatures within range. On a hit, it takes 2d8 Piercing damage and is Bleeding 1. An affected creature makes a Physique saving throw at the end of each of their turns. On a success, they lose the Bleeding condition.
