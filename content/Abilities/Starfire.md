| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | --------------- |
| 1        | 2            | Ins/Sou        | 60 feet         |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area**        |
| -        | -            | Innate         | Sphere (5-foot) |

You call celestial flame upon your foes. Target a point you can see within range. Each creature within a 5-foot Sphere centred on that point must make an Agility saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 1d6 Radiant damage. On a successful save, it takes half damage.
