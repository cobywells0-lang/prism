**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Halberd]], [[Meteor Hammer]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1        | 2            | Agi/Phy        | Self                     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area**                 |
| 4/LR     | -            | Weapon         | Cone Emanation (10-foot) |

With two Actions you swing wildly at a group of foes. Each creature within a 10-foot Cone Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 2d6 Weapon damage. On a success it takes half damage.
