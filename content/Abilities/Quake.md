**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Brawler]], [[Gambler]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats      | Range                    |
| ----- | ------------ | ---------- | ------------------------ |
| 1     | 2            | Phy/Sou    | Self                     |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components** | **Area**                     |
| 2/LR  | -            | Innate     | Cone Emanation (15-foot) |

With 2 Actions you propel a shockwave through the earth. Each creature within a 15-foot Cone Emanation must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d8 Bludgeoning damage and falls Prone. On a success, it takes half damage only.
