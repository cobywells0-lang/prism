**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Brawler]], [[Shifter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range          |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------------- |
| 2        | 3            | Agi/Phy        | Self           |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area**       |
| 2/LR     | -            | Innate         | Line (30-foot) |

With 3 Actions you charge into your foes, barging and trampling as you go. Move in a straight line up to 30 feet. Each creature whose space you move through must make a Physique saving throw. On a failed save the target takes 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed up to 5 feet in a direction of your choosing. On a successful save it takes half damage only.
