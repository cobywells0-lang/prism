*Any Slashing Melee Weapon

As an Action you imbue your weapon with four arcane motes of elemental air. When you cast this ability, and as an Action for the duration, you can expend one mote to make a Ranged Magic attack against one creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Thunder damage. This ability ends after 1 Minute, or when all four motes are expended.
