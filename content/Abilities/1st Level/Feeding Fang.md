**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Shifter]], Species: [[Naga]], [[Skitterling]], [[Vampire]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats             | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 2            | Physique/Instinct | 5 feet   |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**    | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Innate            | -        |

With two Actions you bite down on a foe, rejuvenating yourself with its flesh. Make a melee attack against one creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Piercing damage. You regain Hit Points equal to the Piercing damage dealt.
