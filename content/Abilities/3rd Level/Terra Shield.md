**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Artisan]], [[Brawler]], [[Hunter]], [[Magus]], [[Shifter]], [[Sorcerer]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats                 | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 3        | 2             | Instinct/Intelligence | Self                     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**   | **Components**        | **Area**                 |
| 3/LR     | 1 Minute (1A) | Magic                 | Cone Emanation (30-foot) |

With 2 Actions you telekinetically lift rubble and suspend it defensively in front of you. You gain a pool of 7 d8 dice. Whenever you would take damage, you can expend any number of these dice, roll them, and reduce the damage taken by the total rolled. When you expend the last of these dice, the ability ends.

When you Sustain this ability, you can choose to expel the remaining rubble with a telekinetic blast. Make a ranged magic attack against a number of different creatures within a 30-foot Cone up to the number of dice you have remaining in your pool. On a hit, a target takes 1d8 Bludgeoning damage. Once you make these attacks, the ability ends.
