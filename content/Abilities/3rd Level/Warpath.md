**Prerequisites:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Brawler]], [[Shifter]], [[Swashbuckler]], [[Warrior]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats            | Range          |
| -------- | ------------ | ---------------- | -------------- |
| 3        | 2            | Physique/Agility | Self           |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**   | **Area**       |
| 3/LR     | -            | Innate           | Line (30-foot) |

With 3 Actions you charge into your foes, barging and trampling as you go. Move in a straight line up to 30 feet. Each creature whose space you move through must make a Physique saving throw. On a failed save the target takes 2d10 Bludgeoning damage, is pushed up to 5 feet in a direction of your choosing, and falls Prone. On a successful save it takes half damage only.
