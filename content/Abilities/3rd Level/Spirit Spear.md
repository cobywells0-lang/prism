**Prerequisites:** Ability: [[Familiar Spirit]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats             | Range          |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------------- | -------------- |
| 3        | 2            | Soul/Intelligence | -              |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components**    | **Area**       |
| 3/LR     | -            | Magic             | Line (30-foot) |

With 2 Actions while you can communicate telepathically with your Familiar, you weaponize its arcane form by having it propel through your foes. Each creature within a 30-foot Line Emanation originating from your Familiar must make a Soul saving throw. On a failure it takes 3d10 Corruption damage. If only one creature fails this saving throw, it takes an additional 1d10 Corruption damage and is Slow 2 until the end of its next turn. Your Familiar is then shunted to the space at the far end of the line.
