**Prerequisite:** Class: [[Arcanist]], [[Commander]], [[Gambler]], [[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Witch]], [[Zealot]]

| Level    | Cast Actions  | Stats             | Range           |
| -------- | ------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| 3        | 2             | Soul/Intelligence | 30 feet         |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**   | **Components**    | **Area**        |
| 3/LR     | 1 Minute (1A) | Magic             | Sphere (5-foot) |
With 2 Actions you reach out and attempt to bind a foe's spirit. One creature within range must make a Soul saving throw. On a failure it is Cursed for up to 1 Minute. While Cursed in this way, it cannot willingly move outside of a 5-foot Sphere centred on the space it was originally Cursed. If the target is forced to move outside of this Sphere, it loses the Cursed condition. Each time you Sustain this ability, the target repeats the saving throw, losing the Cursed condition on a success.
