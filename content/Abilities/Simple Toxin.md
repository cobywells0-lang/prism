**Prerequisites:** Tool: Poison Kit

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 1            | Ins/Int        | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | 1 Minute     | Tool, Weapon*  | -        |

*Any Slashing or Piercing Weapon or piece of Ammunition.

As an Action you coat a weapon or piece of ammunition with a basic poison. The next time you damage a creature with that weapon or piece of ammunition, the target must make a Physique saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 2d6 Vitalic damage and is Sickened 1 for 1 minute. On a success it takes half damage only. An affected creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the Sickened condition on a success.
