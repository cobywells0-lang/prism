**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Buckler]], [[Crossbow]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Kama]], [[Rapier]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 2            | Agi/Phy        | 5 Feet   |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Weapon         | -        |

With 2 Actions you strike at the head with the blunt end of your weapon. One creature within 5 feet must make an Physique saving throw. On a failure, the target takes 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and is [[Dazed]] 1 until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage only.
