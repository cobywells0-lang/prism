| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | R            | Agi/Phy        | 5 Feet   |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Weapon         | -        |

You finish a ruthless string of attacks with a crippling blow. As a Reaction after hitting a target with two consecutive attacks, make a melee weapon attack against that creature with Advantage. On a hit the target takes 3d6 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed 20 feet directly away from you.
