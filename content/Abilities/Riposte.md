**Prerequisites:** Weapon: [[Arming Sword]], [[Dagger]], [[Gauntlet]], [[Kama]], [[Rapier]]

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | R            | Agi/Fin        | Weapon   |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 4/LR     | -            | Weapon         | -        |

You capitalise on a failed attack. As a Reaction when a creature you can see misses you with a melee attack, make a melee weapon attack against that creature. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Weapon damage and has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.
