## Level 1: Primal Energy

You have 4 Primal Energy points, which you can expend to empower certain Shifter abilities. You regain a number of Primal Energy points equal to your Instinct bonus when you finish a Short Rest, and regain all expended Primal Energy points when you finish a Long Rest. The maximum number of Primal Energy points you have increases when you reach certain Shifter levels, as shown in the Primal Energy column of the Shifter Core Features table.

## Level 1: Bestial Weapons

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 1            | Phy/Ins        | 5ft      |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| -        | -            | Innate         | -        |

You bite, claw, beat with a tail, or make some other simple animalistic attack. Make an attack roll against one creature within range. On a hit, it takes 1d8 damage. The damage type of this damage is your choice of Slashing, Piercing or Bludgeoning.

If you expend one Primal Energy point to empower this attack, the target instead takes 2d8 damage and must make a Physique saving throw on a hit. On a failure, it suffers an effect depending on the damage type this attack deals.

| Damage Type | Effect                                                                                                                      |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Slashing    | The target is Bleeding 1. It repeats this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, losing this condition on a success. |
| Piercing    | The target is Slow 2 until the end of its next turn.                                                                        |
| Bludgeoning | The target is pushed up to 5 feet away and knocked Prone.                                                                   |

## Level 2: Natural Armour

You gain scales, a shell, thick hide, or some other protective anatomy. While you are not wearing Armour, you gain Damage Reduction 2 against Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage. This Damage Reduction increases at Shifter levels 5 (3), 9 (4) and 13 (5).

## Level 2: Swiftness

Your bestial features grant you greater mobility. Your Speed increases by 5 feet. This bonus increases when you reach Shifter levels 6 (+10 feet) and 11 (+15 feet).

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Shifter levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Shifter Subclass

You gain a Shifter subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Shifter levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Shifter level or lower.

## Level 4: Self Preservation

Your body moves to avoid danger before you consciously recognise the threat. You can use your Instinct bonus in place of your Agility bonus when forced to make an Agility saving throw.

## Level 5: Pre-emptive Strike

You pounce as soon as prey reveals itself. Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can move up to your Speed and make a 1 Action Attack.

## Level 7: Bloodsense

You easily pick up the scent of weakness. You have Advantage on Perception and Survival checks made to find Bloodied creatures, and Bloodied creatures within 30 feet of you cannot be Hidden from you.

## Level 8: Elemental Armour

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | R            | -              | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 1/LR     | -            | Innate         | -        |

Your body’s defences adapt to the environment you are in. As a Reaction when you take Fire, Frost, Lightning, Thunder, Radiant or Acid damage, you gain Damage Reduction equal to your Natural Armour Damage Reduction against that damage type. This Damage Reduction lasts until you Cast this Ability again. Once you Cast this Ability, you cannot Cast it again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 9: Extra Action

You become more fluid and decisive in combat. You can take four Actions on your turn, instead of three.
