## Level 1: Opportunistic

Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack, you can create an opportunity to strike for an ally. Choose one ally you can see. That ally has Advantage on the first attack it makes against your target before the start of your next turn.
## Level 2: Roguish Knack

Your resourcefulness has provided you with a set of tools to help make the best of bad situations, and to exploit opportunities that arise. You can use this feature twice, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You gain an additional use when you reach Knave levels 7 (3 total) and 12 (4 total). You start with one way to use your Roguish Knack:

**Quick Wit.** As a Reaction when you or a creature you can see within 60 feet fails a Check, you can cause the Check to be rolled again, potentially turning a failure into a success.

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. You then gain one Mastery of your choice, which you meet the prerequisites for.

You gain this ability again at Knave levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Knave Subclass

You gain a Knave subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Knave levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Knave level or lower.
