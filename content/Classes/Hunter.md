## Level 2: Swiftness

Your time in the wilds has granted you greater mobility. Your Speed increases by 5 feet. This bonus increases when you reach Hunter level 6 (10 feet).

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. You then gain one Mastery of your choice, which you meet the prerequisites for.

You gain this ability again at Hunter levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Hunter Subclass

You gain a Hunter subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Hunter levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Hunter level or lower.
