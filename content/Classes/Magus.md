## Level 1: Sigil Stance

You learn to enhance your martial skills using a type of runic magic called Sigils. You know one Sigil of your choice, and learn more when you reach certain Magus levels, as shown in the Sigils column of the Magus Core Features table.

Your Sigil Stance ends early if you are [[Incapacitated]], or if you Cast another Sigil.

## Level 2: Swiftness

Your arcane training grants you greater mobility. Your Speed increases by 5 feet.

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Magus levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Magus Subclass

You gain a Magus subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Magus levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Magus level or lower.
