## Level 1: Relic

You choose an object to represent your devotion, and channel the gifts you receive in kind. You gain one of the following options.

**Blessed Weapon.** You are granted a weapon you are Proficient with, which is decorated with the likeness or motifs of your patron. When you use this weapon to perform a Weapon ability, you can use your Soul bonus in place of either your Finesse or Agility bonus (but not both).

**Blessed Icon.** You are granted a special symbol tied to your patron, which can take the form of robes, a banner, or painting (which can be upon the face of a Shield). While you bear this icon, you and all Allies within a 10-foot Sphere Emanation have Advantage on Soul saving throws.

**Blessed Trinket.** You are granted an amulet, crown, statuette, or other small accessory bearing your patron’s motifs. While you bear this trinket, your Magic abilities gain a bonus to one damage and healing roll equal to your Soul bonus.

If your Relic is lost or destroyed, you can gain a replacement by performing a special ritual as an Activity during a Long Rest. When you gain this replacement, the original Relic is destroyed, if it still exists.

Whenever you gain a Zealot level, you can exchange your chosen Relic for one of the other options.

## Level 2: Otherworldly Spark

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range                    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| -        | 2            | Physique/Soul  | Self                     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area**                 |
| 4/LR     | -            | Innate         | Cone Emanation (30-foot) |

With two Actions you present your Relic and inspire awe in both friend and foe. Each Enemy within a 30-foot Cone Emanation must make a Soul saving throw, becoming Charmed or Frightened (your choice) for 1 minute on a failure. An affected creature repeats this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, losing the condition on a success. Each Ally within a 20-foot Sphere Emanation that is Frightened loses that condition.

You can cast this ability four times, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Zealot levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Zealot Subclass

You gain a Zealot subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Zealot levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Zealot level or lower.

Infernal, Celestial, Eldritch, Fey

## Level 14: Divine Intervention
| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| -        | 3            | -              | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 1/LR     | -            | Innate         | -        |

With three Actions you can call upon your patron to work miracles. You cast any Zealot Ritual you meet the prerequisites for as part of casting this ability, instead of during a Long Rest. Once you cast this ability, you cannot cast it again until you finish a Long Rest.
