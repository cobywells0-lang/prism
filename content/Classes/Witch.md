## Level 1: Hex


| Cast Actions | Sustain Actions      | Stats                 | Range    |
| ------------ | -------------------- | --------------------- | -------- |
| 1            | -                    | Instinct/Intelligence | 60 feet  |
| **Uses**     | **Sustain Duration** | **Components**        | **Area** |
| 2/LR         | 1 Minute             | Magic                 | -        |

You can curse your foes to wither. As an Action you can Hex one creature within range. When you cast Hex, and each time you Sustain it, the target takes 1d8 Corruption damage. An affected creature can break the curse by making a Soul saving throw with two Actions. On a success the Hex ends. 

You can Cast this ability twice, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You gain an additional Casting when you reach Witch levels 5 (3), 9 (4) and 13 (5).

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Witch levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Witch Subclass

You gain a Witch subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Witch levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Witch level or lower.

Hedgewitch, Occultist, Seer, Cursebringer
