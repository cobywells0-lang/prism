## Level 1: Duel Me!

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| -        | 1            | Agi/Sou        | 60 Feet  |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 1/SR     | 1 Minute     | Innate         | -        |

As an Action you call out a creature you can see within range. The target must make an Instinct saving throw. On a failure, the target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you, it takes 1d6 Psychic damage whenever it damages a creature other than you, and you deal additional damage equal to your Soul bonus when you hit it with a Weapon attack. An affected creature repeats this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, and whenever you damage a creature other than it, ending the effect on a success.

## Level 2: Adrenaline

The thrill of a messy fight pushes you to your prime. When you reduce an enemy to 0 Hit Points, score a Critical Hit on an enemy, or are forced to make a saving throw, you gain a stack of Adrenaline, up to a maximum number equal to half your Swashbuckler level. 

Immediately after you hit a creature with an attack, you can expend any number of stacks of Adrenaline to deal an additional 1d10 damage per stack expended.
## Level 2: Adrenaline Rush 

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| -        | R            | Agi/Sou        | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| 2/SR     | -            | Innate         | -        |

As a Reaction when you gain a stack of Adrenaline, you can gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Ability bonus and can move up to your Speed. You can cast this Ability twice, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

## Level 2: Swiftness

Your charismatic vigour grants you greater mobility. Your Speed increases by 5 feet. This bonus increases when you reach Swashbuckler level 6 (+10 feet).

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Swashbuckler levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Swashbuckler Subclass

You gain a Swashbuckler subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Swashbuckler levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Swashbuckler level or lower.

## Level 4: Sea Legs

You gain a Swim and Climb speed of 20 feet. If you already have either speed, the speeds you have are instead increased by 10 feet.

## Level 5: Roguish Luck

You have a +1 bonus to all saving throws. This bonus increases to +2 at Swashbuckler level 12.

| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |

As an Action you can show off with a flashy demonstration of skill. You expend three stacks of Adrenaline and regain one expended Exertion Die.

## Level 12: Daring Escape

If the movement you make as part of using your Adrenaline Rush ability results in you no longer being within the range or the area of effect of the ability that forced you to make the saving throw, you automatically succeed it.
