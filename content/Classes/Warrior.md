## Level 1: Battle Stamina

Your martial training allows you to reserve your strength and push on when others falter.

As an Action you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to 5 plus twice your Warrior level. If any of these Temporary Hit Points remain after 1 minute, they disappear.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a Short or Long Rest. You gain an additional use of this feature at Warrior level 9 (2 uses) and regain all expended uses when you complete a Short or Long Rest.

## Level 2: Weapon Master

You are familiar with weapons of all shapes and sizes. You can train with a weapon you lack Proficiency with as an Activity during a Long Rest. You exchange one Weapon Proficiency you have with Proficiency with the weapon you trained with.

## Level 2: Swiftness

Your rigorous training grants you greater mobility. Your Speed increases by 5 feet.

## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Warrior levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Warrior Subclass

You gain a Warrior subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Warrior levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Warrior level or lower.

Gladiator, Cavalier, Samurai, Crusader 

## Level 4: Practiced Techniques

| Level      | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| ---------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| -          | -            | Fin/Phy        | Self     |
| **Uses**   | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| Ability/LR | -            | Innate         | -        |

You’ve practiced advanced techniques until they feel like second nature. Choose two Level 1 Weapon Abilities you know. They become your Practiced Techniques. You can cast your Practiced Techniques without expending their uses by instead expending one use of this ability. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your Ability bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

The maximum level of your Practiced Techniques increase at Warrior level 9 (Level 2) and 13 (Level 3). You can swap one Practiced Technique for another Weapon Ability you know whenever you gain a Warrior level.

## Level 5: Overcome

You are a bastion of reliability on the battlefield. When you fail a saving throw, you can choose to succeed instead. Once you use this Ability, you cannot use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

You gain an additional use of this Ability at Warrior level 13, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 8: Redoubled Offence

When you use your Battle Stamina ability, you can regain half of your expended Hit Points and regain one expended use of all of your Weapon Abilities. Once you use Battle Stamina in this way, you cannot use it thus again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Level 9: Extra Action

You become more fluid and decisive in combat. You can take four Actions on your turn, instead of three. 

## Level 12: Stamina Wellspring

Whenever you roll Initiative, you regain one expended use of Battle Stamina.

## Level 14: Technique Mastery

When you cast a Practiced Technique without expending its use, you can deal an additional 2d12 Vitalic damage to a creature you hit with the attack roll, or an additional 2d6 Vitalic damage to each creature that failed the saving throw you forced.
