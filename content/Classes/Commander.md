## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Commander levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Commander Subclass

You gain a Commander subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Commander levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Commander level or lower.

Captain, Bannerbearer, Conductor, Instructor
