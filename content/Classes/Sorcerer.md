## Level 3: Stat Increase

Increase one of your two lowest stats by +1 (if multiple stats are tied for second lowest, any tied stat is valid). Then increase any one stat of your choice by +1, to a maximum of +5. 

You gain this ability again at Sorcerer levels 7, 11 and 15.

## Level 3: Sorcerer Subclass

You gain a Sorcerer subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Sorcerer levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Sorcerer level or lower.
