You can become Hidden from one or more creatures by hiding as an Action while Concealed from those creatures. While Hidden you experience the following effects:

**Untargetable.** You cannot be the target of abilities unless they are Area abilities.

**Sneak Attack.** You have Advantage on your first attack.

You are no longer Hidden from a creature if you are not Concealed from it, target it with an attack or force it to make a saving throw, or if that creature makes a successful Perception check against your Stealth DC.
