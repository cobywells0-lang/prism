While Restrained, one or more limbs have been bound or otherwise disabled. While a limb is Restrained, it cannot be used to perform abilities. You can become Restrained multiple times, each Restrained condition affecting a separate limb.

| Limb | Potential Uses                                      |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Arm  | Weapon attacks                                      |
| Head | Bites, breath attacks                               |
| Leg  | Walking, swimming                                   |
| Tail | Specific tail attacks, weapon attacks if prehensile |
| Wing | Flying                                              |
For each Restrained leg, your Walking and Swimming Speeds are reduced by 5 feet. If all legs are Restrained, your Walking and Swimming Speeds become 0.

If any wing is Restrained, your Flying Speed becomes 0.
