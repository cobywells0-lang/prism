While Prone, you are knocked off balance. If you are not [[Airborne]], you are laid down. If you are Airborne, your movements are difficult to control. You experience the following effects:

**Outgoing Disadvantage.** All attacks you make are made with Disadvantage.

**Attacks Affected.** Melee attacks targeting you are made with Advantage. Ranged attacks targeting you are made with Disadvantage.

**Crawl.** Your Speed cannot be greater than 5 feet.

While Prone, you can right yourself with 2 Actions. You lose the Prone condition.
