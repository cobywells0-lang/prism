While Exhausted, lingering wounds or overexertion cause you to struggle to perform to your fullest. You suffer the following effects:

**Checks Affected.** Whenever you make a Check you discard any dice that roll equal or below the number of levels of Exhausted before totalling the result.

**Death Spiral.** If you become Exhausted 6, you die.

You lose one level of the Exhausted condition when you finish a Long Rest.
