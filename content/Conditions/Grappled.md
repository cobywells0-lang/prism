While Grappled, you are being held by another creature. You suffer the following effects:

**Held**. You cannot move and you have Disadvantage on weapon attacks made against the grappling creature. 

**Drag.** You can be dragged along with the grappling creature’s movement. The speed of the grappling creature depends on your size relative to it.

| Size Relative to Grappling Creature   | Drag Speed           |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Smaller by at least one size category | Full Speed           |
| Same size category                    | Half Speed           |
| Larger by at least one size category  | Cannot exceed 5 feet |

**Escape.** While Grappled you can attempt to escape by making a Physique or Agility saving throw (DC equal to four times the sum of the grappling creature's Agility and Physique bonuses) as an Action, losing the condition on a success.
