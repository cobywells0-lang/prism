Each plane has a boundary between itself and the void, within which the particular rules of that plane gradually warp and give way to chaos. This borderland is known as the Fade.

The Fade is imperceptible and inaccessible for most creatures, but it is an intrinsic property of each plane of reality, and an explorer with a particular curiosity for the bizarre and incomprehensible may be drawn to the particular methods of accessing this strange realm.

The geography of the Fade is best described as a dream-like shadow of the attached plane. Features and objects from the attached plane may still be recognisable, but they are invariably warped in shape and dimension - sometimes in ways that contradict the normal rules of reality. Colours shift towards hazy, washed-out tones and shades of mist-like grey.

Exotic life is possible in the Fade, but it is sparce. What little ecosystem exists is bizarre and alien.

While in the Fade you are imperceptible to creatures on the attached plane, and immune to all effects originating there. However, you are visible to, and able to interact with, other creatures in the Fade.

Most large objects from the attached plane, such as walls, floors and doors, remain tangible in the Fade. However, their forms may be distorted in ways that allow them to be passed through, or cause them to behave in otherwise unusual ways, at the GM's discretion. Interacting with an object in the Fade - if possible at all - is unlikely to have the same effect on the object in the attached plane.
