An Element Weaver Brawler channels elemental magic to bolster and empower themselves in the fray. They combine powerful spellcasting with martial techniques empowered by flame and frost.

### Level 3: Element Weaver Talents

When you reach a Brawler level specified in the Element Weaver Talents table, you thereafter know the listed talents, and they count as Brawler talents for you. You use Physique and Agility as your stats for these talents, instead of the stats listed in their respective talent tables.

### Level 3: Elemental Arms

As an Action immediately after you cast a Magic talent that deals Acid, Fire, Frost or Lightning damage, you can weave a fragment of that elemental magic into a pair of spectral arms. These Elemental Arms hover over your shoulders for 1 minute, or until you dismiss them (no action required) and the damage type dealt by the triggering talent becomes your Elemental Affinity. If the triggering talent deals more than one valid damage type, you choose which becomes your Elemental Affinity.

As part of the Action taken to summon your Elemental Arms, and as an Action for as long as they remain, you can make an attack with them. Make a melee magic attack against one creature within 10 feet. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Elemental Affinity damage.

You can summon your Elemental Arms twice, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Level 6: Ward of Four Elements

You have Damage Reduction against your Elemental Affinity equal to your Brawler level. While your Elemental Arms are active, you have this Damage Reduction against Acid, Fire, Frost and Lightning damage.

### Level 10: Elemental Enhancement

Your Elemental Arms now deal 2d6 damage on a hit, instead of 1d8. Additionally, you regain one expended use of Elemental Arms when you finish a Short Rest.

### Level 14: Fury of the Elementals

With two Actions you can draw a massive spectral fist up from the ground to uppercut at a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature within a 15-foot-radius, 50-foot-tall True Cylinder must make an Agility saving throw. On a failure, is suffers a unique effect depending on your Elemental Affinity. On a success, it takes half damage only.

| Elemental Affinity | Fury of the Elementals Effect                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Acid               | The target takes 6d8 Acid damage and its AC and Critical Hit Threshold are both reduced by 2 until the start of your next turn.                                              |
| Fire               | The target takes 5d8 Fire damage and is Burning 3. It makes an Agility saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a success, it is no longer Burning.                  |
| Frost              | The target takes 2d8 Frost damage and is Frozen until the end of its next turn.                                                                                              |
| Lightning          | The target takes 5d8 Lightning damage and is Slow 2 and Dazed 1. It makes an Instinct saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a success, it loses these conditions. |
Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a 7th-Level Stamina Point to use it again (no action required).
