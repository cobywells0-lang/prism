In order to play Prism, you must first create your character. Your character is the avatar through whomst you will experience the story you and your friends craft together. Creating your character can be boiled down to a few simple steps.

### Species

Your Species defines what kind of creature you are, determines your size, and grants you a handful of unique abilities. Once you choose your Species, it cannot change outside of extraordinary means, so choose carefully!

Choose one Species, and note your Creature Type, Speed, Size, and all Abilities that Species grants.

### Stats

Your Stats influence how effective you are in a range of areas, from using skills to making attacks and resisting negative effects. You can find a detailed account of how each Stat affects your character [[Stats|here]]. Your Stats will increase regularly as you gain levels.

Choose three Stats to set to +2. The remaining three Stats start at only +1.

### Class

Your Class determines the archetypes your character can fill, and is arguably the most important decision you need to make in terms of shaping how your character can interact with the world.

Choose one Class, and gain everything from that class's Traits Table, as well as any Abilities that level 1 in that class grants.

### Learnt Abilities

At Level 1, you are able to learn 2 Learnt Abilities. These are the abilities that distinguish your character from others of the same species and class. See more information on Learnt Abilities [[Abilities|here]].

### Starting At Higher Levels

When starting at a character level greater than 1, you should typically construct the character as if you started at level 1 and then levelled up to your desired starting level, one level at a time. 

To easily determine how many Learnt Abilities of each level you should know, refer to the appropriate row of the Learnt Abilities By Level table.
