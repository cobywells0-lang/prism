Many Abilities deal damage. When a creature takes damage, it loses that number of Hit Points. There are several types of damage, depending on the nature of the source.

| Damage Type | Description                       | Example Sources                               |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Acid        | Chemical Burns and Corrosion      | Corrosive Liquids, Digestive Enzymes          |
| Anima       | Magical Impact                    | Pure Magic, Arcane Forces                     |
| Bludgeoning | Heavy Blunt Impact                | Blunt Weapons, Falling                        |
| Corrupting  | Draining or Poisoning of the Soul | Curses, Spectres                              |
| Fire        | Heat Burns                        | Fire, Lava, Hot Metal                         |
| Frost       | Frostbite, Cold Burns             | Blizzards, Ice                                |
| Lightning   | Electrocution                     | Storms, Electricity                           |
| Mental      | Draining or Poisoning of the Mind | Psychic Attacks, Trauma                       |
| Piercing    | Puncture by a Point               | Stabbing and Ranged Weapons, Teeth and Spikes |
| Slashing    | Lacerations and Slicing           | Sharp Weapons, Claws                          |
| Thunder     | Concussion, Pressure              | Explosions, Sonic Weapons                     |
| Vitalic     | Draining or Poisoning of the Body | Poisons, Venom, Overexertion                  |
| Radiant     | Light and Radiation Burns         | Bright Light, Lasers, Radiation               |
### Multiple Damage Types

If an Ability is capable of dealing several types of damage, they will all be listed like so: 

Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing

You can choose which damage type the Ability deals each time you cast it.

### Weapon Damage

An Ability may call for Weapon damage. In this case, the Ability deals damage of the same type as the Weapon used to cast it. If the Weapon can deal multiple types of damage, you choose which of those types the Ability uses.
