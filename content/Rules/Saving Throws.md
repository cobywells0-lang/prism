Some abilities call for saving throws in order to determine to what extent they affect you. There are six types of saving throws, one for each of the [[Stats]].

In order to make a saving throw you must make a [[Checks|check]] using your bonus for the relevant stat and your Proficiency bonus. The DC is set by the ability that called for the saving throw. You can always choose to fail a saving throw.

For example, take an ability that calls for a DC 12 Soul saving throw. If targeted, you would sum your Soul bonus (let's say +2) and Proficiency bonus (also +2 here), and roll a number of D6 equal to the total - that's 4d6. If we roll 12 or higher, we succeed the saving throw. If we roll 11 or lower, we fail. The ability will state what happens if we succeed or fail.

### Difficulty Class
The DC of a saving throw is determined by the Ability bonus of the triggering ability. It is four multiplied by the Ability bonus.
