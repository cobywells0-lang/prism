Impacts with the terrain, due to falling or being pushed, can be a significant hazard to Heroes and Monsters.

**Falling.** When you fall to the ground from a height of 10 feet or more, you take 1d6 Bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet fallen.

**Pushing.** When a solid obstacle prevents you from being pushed the full distance, you take 1d6 Bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet of push cut short, provided you are first pushed at least 10 feet. If you are stopped by an obstacle before moving 10 feet, you take no damage. For example, if you are pushed 30 feet, but hit a wall after 10 feet of movement, the remaining 20 feet of push is converted into 2d6 Bludgeoning damage.
