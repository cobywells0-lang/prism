There are times a player may want to get their way through social means, and for high-stakes social play you may wish to give the encounter more weight and gameplay than a single skill check. In these cases, that character may engage in a Social Encounter with the target. A Social Encounter can be played on its own, or during a Combat Encounter, depending on the situation.

**Language.** In order to engage in a Social Encounter with a creature, the player character must share at least one Language proficiency with the creature in question.

**Patience.** The targeted creature has an amount of Patience equal to its Intelligence bonus. If its Patience is reduced to zero, the Social Encounter is broken off by the target, who refuses to engage in further debate, and the results of the encounter are determined.

**Stubbornness.** The targeted creature has an amount of Stubbornness equal to its Soul bonus. If its Stubbornness is reduced to zero, the target relents and grants the player character the best outcome it is reasonably able to provide. If the Social Encounter is broken off before the target’s Stubbornness reaches zero, the target may offer a compromise, or not offer anything at all, depending on how much its Stubbornness was reduced during the encounter.

**Ideals.** Ideals are generalised beliefs and desires. Most creatures you can engage in a Social Encounter hold two Ideals in High Standing, and two in Low Standing. The ideals are as follows:

Wealth, Health, Freedom, Justice, Revelry, Power, Knowledge, Charity, Community, Faith, Tradition, Honour, Beauty, Nature.

**Social Actions.** As an Action (once per turn, if during Initiative), a player character can attempt a Persuasion, Deception or Intimidation check, choosing an Ideal to focus their argument upon. On a success, the target’s Stubbornness is reduced by 1, or by 2 if they chose an Ideal the target holds in High Standing. If an Ideal was chosen that the target holds in Low Standing, the check is automatically failed. Regardless of if the check is a success or failure, the target’s Patience is reduced by 1.

The DC of these checks is 4 times the target’s Instinct plus Proficiency bonuses if attempting a Deception or Intimidation check, and 4 times the target’s Soul plus Proficiency bonuses if attempting a Persuasion check.

**Consequences.** Beyond the mechanics of a Social Encounter, the choices the player makes will often have an impact on how the target sees them, and how the target may act going forwards. Relying on Intimidation will very often have downstream consequences - an intimidated guard may inform their superior once the threat has passed, or a frightened thug might harbour a vendetta and plot against the party. The consequences of Deception may take longer to come about, but once the lies are brought to light the target is likely to be distrustful of the party, and a particularly prideful creature may swear to take revenge. Beyond this, collaboration with a powerful figure is likely to gain the attention of that figure’s opposition.
