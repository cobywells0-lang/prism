A Condition is an altered state that affects a creature in a particular way. Some Conditions, such as [[Bleeding]] and [[Frightened]], are purely negative, and are usually inflicted by specific Talents, features or monster attacks. Others, such as [[Airborne]] and [[Bloodied]], arise from more general circumstances and may affect a creature positively, negatively, or not at all.

The duration of most Conditions is specified by the Talent, feature, monster attack or circumstance that applies them.

### Stacking

Each Condition is classified as either **Binary**, **Stacking** or **Non-Stacking**. This classification determines how multiple applications of the same Condition interact.

**Binary.** A Binary Condition does not have stacks. A creature is either affected by the Condition or it isn't. If multiple sources apply the same Binary Condition, the creature remains affected until all sources have ended.

**Stacking.** When a Stacking Condition is applied to a creature, the number of stacks applied is added to any existing stacks of that Condition. For example, if you inflict Bleeding 1 on a creature that is already Bleeding 1, it becomes Bleeding 2.

**Non-Stacking.** When a Non-Stacking Condition is applied to a creature, its stacks are not added to any existing stacks of that Condition. Instead, the creature is affected by the highest number of stacks applied by any active source. For example, if you inflict [[Dazed]] 2 on a creature that is already Dazed 1, it becomes Dazed 2. If the source applying Dazed 2 subsequently ends, but the source applying Dazed 1 remains active, the creature becomes Dazed 1 again.

### Source

The source of a Condition is the Talent, feature, monster attack or circumstance that applies it. Applications of the same Condition by different creatures, or through different Talents, features or attacks, are considered separate sources.

Repeated applications of the same Condition by the same creature using the same Talent, feature or attack are considered to originate from the same source.

Each source of a Condition is tracked separately. When a Condition ends with respect to a particular source, whether through a successful saving throw, the expiration of its duration, or another effect, all stacks originating from that source are removed simultaneously, unless otherwise specified. Any stacks originating from other sources remain unaffected.

For example, if a Shifter inflicts Bleeding 3 on a creature through repeated uses of [[Shifter|Bestial Weapons]], and that creature subsequently succeeds on a saving throw to end the Bleeding inflicted by Bestial Weapons, it loses all three stacks.

However, if a creature is Bleeding 2 from a Shifter's Bestial Weapons and Bleeding 1 from [[Piercing Shot]], it has a total of three stacks. If it subsequently succeeds on a saving throw to end the Bleeding inflicted by Bestial Weapons, but not Piercing Shot, it becomes Bleeding 1.

The same principle applies to Non-Stacking Conditions. When one source ends, the creature is affected by the highest number of stacks among any remaining active sources.
