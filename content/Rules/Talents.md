Talents are self-contained descriptions of feats you are capable of performing reliably and consistently. While they do not represent the absolute limit of what you are capable of, they are the main mechanic by which you can customise your Hero. 

### Talent Table

Every Talent has an associated table of information which is pertinent to the use of that Talent, called the Talent Table. A typical Talent Table looks like this:

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats    | Range |
| ----- | ------------ | -------- | ----- |
|       |              |          |       |
| **Type**  | **Duration**     | **Somatics** | **Area**  |
|       |              |          |       |

**Level.** The Level of the Talent determines the minimum level of Stamina point that must be expended in order to cast it. A 0th level Talent requires no Stamina point be expended in order to cast.

**Cast Actions.** The Cast Actions of the Talent describes how many consecutive Actions must be spent in order to Cast the Talent. If it is cast as a Reaction, the body text of the Talent describes what may trigger the Reaction.

**Stats.** The Stats of the Talent determines which Stat Bonuses you use when calculating the [[Talent Bonus]] of that Talent.

**Range.** The Range of the Talent describes how far away from yourself the Talent can affect. This is often used for determining the Range of attacks, or how far away the origin point of an Area effect can be.

**Type.** The Type of the Talent determines how it interacts with other Talents and features, as well as determining additional conditions for casting the Talent. The Types are as follows:

	**Innate.** An Innate Talent does not depend upon any external factors. You can always cast an Innate Talent, provided you fulfil other listed prerequisites.

	**Magic.** A Magic Talent depends upon unseen arcane forces. Some effects or environments may interfer with the casting of a Magic Talent.

	**Weapon.** A Weapon Talent requires the use of a specific weapon. In order to cast a Weapon Talent, you must currently be holding a weapon listed in the Talent prerequisites, with which you are proficient.

	**Tool.** A Tool Talent requires the use of a specific tool kit. In order to cast a Tool Talent, you must currently be holding a tool kit listed in the Talent prerequisites, with which you are proficient.

**Duration.** The Duration of the Talent describes the maximum length of time the effects of the Talent last before ending. If a Sustain Action cost is listed alongside the duration, that number of consecutive Actions must be spent each turn in order to maintain the effects of the Talent, which is called Sustaining the Talent. If you do not Sustain a Talent which requires to be Sustained, the Talent ends at the end of that turn.

**Somatics.** The Somatics of the Talent describe what parts of your body you must be able to utilise in order to cast the Talent. What that means exactly is explained below.

| Somatic | Requirement                                                                                                                                             |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arm     | At least one arm must be unrestrained and unburdened. In the case of Weapon and Tool Talents, the associated weapon or tool must be held in that arm    |
| Arms    | At least two arms must be unrestrained and unburdened. In the case of Weapon and Tool Talents, the associated weapon or tool must be held in those arms |
| Leg     | At least one leg must be unrestrained.                                                                                                                  |
| Legs    | All legs must be unrestrained.                                                                                                                          |
| Head    | At least one head must be unrestrained.                                                                                                                 |
| Voice   | You must be able to speak coherently, and must not be silenced.                                                                                         |

**Area.** The Area of the Talent describes the shape and properties of its Area effect, if it has one. There are two properties an Area effect can have.

	**True.** A True Area effect ignores the effects of Partial and Total Cover.

	**Emanation.** An Emanation Area effect is centred on, and moves with, the caster.

### Prerequisites

Many Talents have prerequisites that you must meet before you can learn them.

**Level.** All Talents have a level listed from 0th to 8th. You must have at least one Stamina point of that level in order to learn the Talent.

**Class.** Many Talents have one or more class prerequisites listed. You must be of one of the listed classes in order to learn that Talent. If a Talent has no classes listed, any class may learn it. For characters with levels in multiple classes, see [[Multiclassing]].

**Species.** Some Talents have one or more species listed. If you are of that species, you may learn that ability.

**Proficiency.** Many Talents have one or more weapons, tools or skills listed. You must have Proficiency with at least one of those in order to learn that ability. In order to cast abilities with a weapon or tool prerequisite, you must be holding a weapon or tool that meets that prerequisite, and that you are proficient with.

If a Talent has both a Class and Species prerequisite, you need only fulfil one of the two. If a Talent has a Proficiency prerequisite, it must be fulfilled in addition to any Class or Species prerequisite.

### Stamina Points

In order to Cast a Talent of 1st level or greater, you must expend one Stamina point of the same level as the Talent, or higher. If you have no applicable Stamina points, you cannot Cast that Talent. Some Talents gain additional benefits when cast using a Stamina point of a level greater than the Talent level.
