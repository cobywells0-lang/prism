### Starting Combat
Combat begins immediately after a creature performs an action that would prompt an aggressive response from other creatures. This triggering action may be an attack, in which case that attack is resolved before combat begins.

When combat begins, each creature involved must roll for Initiative. You roll a Check using your Instinct bonus. The result of this check is your Initiative Score

Creatures take their turns one after the other, in descending order of Initiative Scores - highest Initiative Score first. When the final creature in the Initiative order finishes its turn, the next round of combat begins and takes place in the same order.

### Taking a Turn
On your turn, you are able to use [[Action|Actions]] to perform various tasks. You have three Actions.

**Movement.** At the start of your turn you are able to move up to half your Speed (no action required). As an Action, you can move up to your Speed.

**Utilize.** As an Action you can interact with an object in the environment, such as pulling a lever.

**Skill Check.** As an Action (or multiple, if the DM determines the task requires additional time or effort) you can attempt a Skill check.

**Ability.** You can cast Abilities that you know and fulfil the prerequisite criteria for performing, provided you have available uses. Abilities may require one or more Actions to cast.

### Ending Combat
Combat ends when all hostile parties agree to stop fighting, when all members of one party are Incapacitated or dead, or when all involved creatures are no longer able to continue fighting due to distance or other factors.

When combat ends, Initiative Scores are discarded, and all creatures move freely.
