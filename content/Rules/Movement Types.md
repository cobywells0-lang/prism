Whenever you move, you must choose to use one of the movement types you have access to. 

Your speed with that movement type indicates the maximum distance you can move in one Action using that movement type. 

You can always move a distance lesser than your speed if you choose to (for example, you can choose to step only 5 feet on your turn, despite having a walking speed of 20 feet). 

The movement types are as follows:

**Walk.** You stride, run, hop or slither along a flat horizontal surface, or up an easily manageable slope.

**Swim.** You move through water or another thin liquid in any direction.

**Climb.** You move up or across a steep slope, or up a textured vertical surface (like a tree trunk). You cannot easily climb up a smooth vertical surface (like a plain wall) or along the underside of a ceiling.

**Fly.** You move through the air in any direction. You can fly to the ground in order to land safely. While Airborne, you can choose not to fall at the end of your turn if you fly at least 5 feet during that turn.

**Burrow.** You dig through soft dirt, mud or loose rubble.
