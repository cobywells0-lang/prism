Resting is a period of low-activity downtime where you regain your strength. There are two types of rest, which grant different benefits and require different lengths of time to accomplish.
### Long Rest
If you spend at least one day and one night consecutively resting in a safe location like a friendly town, tavern, or home camp, you gain the benefits of a Long Rest. The night must be eight hours spent sleeping, and cannot be interrupted for more than one hour. The day must be a cumulative 16 hours, spent before or after the night, or a mixture of both. A Long Rest grants the following benefits:

**Hit Points.** You regain all expended Hit Points.

**Hit Point Dice.** You regain all expended Hit Point Dice.

**Exertion Dice.** You regain all expended Exertion Dice.

**Abilities.** Many Abilities regain some or all of their uses when you finish a Long Rest.
### Short Rest
If you spend at least one consecutive hour resting in a reasonably safe location like a locked room or temporary camp, you gain the benefits of a Short Rest. While resting you can perform no task more strenuous than keeping watch. A Short Rest grants the following benefits:

**Hit Points.** You can expend any number of Hit Point Dice to roll them. Add your Physique bonus to the result of each dice rolled and regain that many expended Hit Points.

**Abilities.** Many Abilities regain some or all of their uses when you finish a Short Rest.
