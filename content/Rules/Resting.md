Resting is a period of low-activity downtime where you regain your strength. While resting you can perform no task more strenuous than keeping watch. There are two types of rest, which grant different benefits and require different lengths of time to accomplish.
### Long Rest
If you spend at least one day and one night consecutively resting in a safe location like a friendly town, tavern, or home camp, you gain the benefits of a Long Rest. The night must be eight hours spent sleeping, and cannot be interrupted for more than one hour. The day must be a cumulative 16 hours, spent before or after the night, or a mixture of both. A Long Rest grants the following benefits:

**Hit Points.** You regain all expended Hit Points.

**Hit Point Dice.** You regain all expended Hit Point Dice.

**Exertion Dice.** You regain all expended Exertion Dice.

**Stamina.** You regain all expended Stamina points.

**Features.** Some class and species features have limited uses which may be regained.
### Short Rest
If you spend at least one consecutive hour resting in a reasonably safe location like a locked room or temporary camp, you gain the benefits of a Short Rest. A Long Rest that lasted more than one hour, but was interrupted before the required duration, may also grant the benefits of a Short Rest. A Short Rest grants the following benefits:

**Hit Points.** You can expend any number of Hit Point Dice to roll them. Add your Physique bonus to the result of each dice rolled and regain that many expended Hit Points.

**Features.** Some class and species features have limited uses which may be regained.
