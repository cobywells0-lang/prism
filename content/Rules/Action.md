An Action is a resource used to move, cast most Abilities, interact with the environment, and attempt Skill checks. It represents the speed at which you can perform tasks.

If an Ability or Skill check requires more than one Action, each of those actions must be spent consecutively and on the same turn. 

You have three Actions, and regain all used Actions at the start of your turn. Unspent Actions are lost at the end of your turn.
