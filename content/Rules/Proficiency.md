
You can become Proficient with specific Weapons, Tools, Skills and Languages. You gain a number of Proficiencies, additional to those your Class provides, equal to your Intelligence bonus. Proficiencies gained in this way can be Weapons, Tools, Skills, Languages, or any combination thereof.

**Weapons and Tools.** Proficiency with a Weapon or Tool allows you to learn Abilities that use that item. When you deal damage with an Ability that uses a Weapon or Tool you have Proficiency with, you deal additional damage equal to your Proficiency bonus for each Action that Ability takes to cast.

**Skills.** Proficiency with a Skill allows you to add your Proficiency bonus when making checks with that Skill.

**Languages.** Proficiency with a Language allows you to read, write, speak and understand that language fluently.
