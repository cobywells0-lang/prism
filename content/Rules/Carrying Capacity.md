Your size and Endurance bonus determine the maximum weight in kilograms that you can sustainably carry, as shown here. Your size and Might bonus determine the maximum weight you can drag, lift, or push. If your carrying capacity exceeds your drag, lift, or push capacity, you can use your carrying capacity for those purposes instead.

While dragging, lifting or pushing weight in excess of your Carry capacity, your Speed cannot exceed 5 feet and you gain one level of Exhaustion for every minute you do so, unless you take at least one minute to rest after each minute of work.

| Size     | Carry              | Drag/Lift/Push |
| -------- | ------------------ | -------------- |
| Small    | 7.5kg x Endurance  | 20kg x Might   |
| Medium   | 10kg x Endurance   | 30kg x Might   |
| Large    | 50kg x Endurance   | 150kg x Might  |
| Huge     | 200kg x Endurance  | 600kg x Might  |
| Colossal | 1000kg x Endurance | 3000kg x Might |
