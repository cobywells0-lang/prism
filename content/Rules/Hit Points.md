You have a maximum number of Hit Points at level 1 equal to the size of your class’s Hit Point Die plus your Physique bonus. Whenever you gain a level, your Maximum Hit Points increases by a roll of your class’s Hit Point Die (or the average, rounded down) plus your Physique bonus.

When you take damage, you lose that many Hit Points. If you ever fall to 0 Hit Points, you fall Unconscious and gain a level of Exhaustion. While you have 0 Hit Points, you gain a level of Exhaustion whenever you take damage. You regain consciousness if you regain any number of Hit Points, and you automatically regain a number of Hit Points equal to your Character Level after 1 hour.
