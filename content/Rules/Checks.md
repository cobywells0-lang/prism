Checks are how most things in Prism are resolved. A Check is rolled when you make an attack, attempt a skill, or are forced to make a saving throw.

In order to make a Check, you first total the bonuses you have for that roll, and then roll a number of D6 dice equal to that total. The total of the results of the dice is the total value of the roll, and is usually compared to a set Difficulty Class. If the roll total is equal to, or greater than, the Difficulty Class, the roll is successful.

For example, if you wanted to lift a heavy obstacle, you would roll a Might check. As Might is a Physique Skill, the first bonus is simply your Physique bonus (let’s say you have +1). If you are Proficient in Might, you would also add your Proficiency Bonus (we’ll use +2 here). That gives you a total of +3, meaning you roll 3d6. If the total of that roll is greater than or equal to the Difficulty Class of the check, you succeed in lifting the obstacle.

### Tasks

When a Check is too difficult to reliably achieve in a couple of attempts, but can be repeated infinitely without interruption - for example, in the case of picking a difficult lock or deciphering a complex rune - it may make more sense to treat that check as a Task. In this case, find the check bonus and DC in the table below to determine the duration of the Task. Instead of rolling, the check is succeeded after that duration has elapsed, provided the Hero spends the entirety of that time uninterrupted and working on the Task.


**Table of DC Range of Tasks of Varying Duration**

| Check Bonus | 1 Minute | 10 Minutes | 1 Hour | 8 Hours |
| ----------- | -------- | ---------- | ------ | ------- |
| +3          | 14-17    | 18         | -      | -       |
| +4          | 18-21    | 22-23      | 24     | -       |
| +5          | 22-25    | 26-27      | 28-29  | 30      |
| +6          | 26-30    | 31-32      | 33-34  | 35      |
| +7          | 30-34    | 35-36      | 37-39  | 40      |
| +8          | 33-38    | 39-41      | 42-43  | 44      |
| +9          | 37-42    | 43-45      | 46-48  | 49      |
| +10         | 41-46    | 47-50      | 51-52  | 53-54   |

Checks with a DC lower than those shown in the 1 Minute column may also be performed as 1-Minute Tasks, though attempting them as normal Checks may be quicker. Checks with a DC greater than the highest listed DC for their Check Bonus cannot be completed as Tasks, even if succeeding on a normal Check would be theoretically possible.
