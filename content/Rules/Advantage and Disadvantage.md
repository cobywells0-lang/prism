Some situations may allow you to roll a Check with Advantage, or force you to roll it with Disadvantage. 

When rolling with Advantage, you roll an additional D6. When rolling with Disadvantage, you roll one fewer D6. 

If you have both Advantage and Disadvantage on a roll, you roll only with the one you’re gaining from the greatest number of sources. Regardless of the number of sources you gain Advantage or Disadvantage from, you roll only one additional or one fewer D6. If both are gained equally, you roll with neither.
