Abilities are self-contained descriptions of feats you are capable of performing reliably and consistently. While they do not represent the absolute limit of what you are capable of, they are the main mechanic by which you can customise your character. Abilities fall under one of three categories.

### Core Class Abilities.

Core Class Abilities are Abilities that are granted to you at certain class levels, as specified in the Class Abilities column of the respective class features table. Core Class Abilities do not count towards the number of Abilities you can learn, and are always available to you once you have reached the prerequisite level.

### Core Species Abilities

Core Species Abilities are abilities that are granted to you by your species. They are listed under your species, and are granted at Level 1 unless otherwise specified.

### Learnt Abilities

Learnt Abilities are Abilities that are not automatically available to you, that you must first learn in order to use. The number of Abilities you can learn at a given level is specified in the Learnt Abilities column of any class features table. 

When you gain a level, you can replace one Learnt Ability you know with another you meet the prerequisites for. When you replace an ability, the new ability must be of a level equal to or less than the level of the replaced ability.

#### Prerequisites

Many Learnt Abilities have prerequisites that you must meet before you can learn them.

**Level.** All Learnt Abilities have a level listed from 1 to 8. Your character level, halved and rounded up, must be equal to or greater than an ability's level in order to learn that ability. 

**Class.** Many Learnt Abilities have one or more classes listed. You must be of one of the listed classes in order to learn that ability. If an ability has no classes listed, any class may learn it. For characters with levels in multiple classes, see [[Multiclassing]].

**Species.** Some Learnt Abilities have one or more species listed. If you are of that species, you may learn that ability. Species takes precedence over Class prerequisites, but not Level.

**Proficiency.** Many Learnt Abilities have one or more weapons, tools or skills listed. You must have Proficiency with at least one of those in order to learn that ability. In order to cast abilities with a weapon or tool prerequisite, you must be holding a weapon or tool that meets that prerequisite, and that your are proficient with.

### Basic Abilities

Basic Abilities are Abilities that do not need to be learnt, and are instead made available by holding the weapon or tool that the Basic Ability belongs to.

If the item is a Simple weapon, you are always able to cast its Basic Ability. If the item is a Specialist weapon or a tool, you must have Proficiency with that weapon or tool in order to cast its Basic Ability.

You can never cast the Basic Ability of a weapon or tool you are not holding.
