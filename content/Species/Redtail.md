Redtails are a nomadic avian race, with powerful wings and their large, namesake tail plume. Despite being capable of flight, they prefer to travel long distances by foot for two reasons: It conserves energy, and they’ve yet to figure out how to make their caravans fly alongside them.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid Beast
**Size:** Small or Medium  
**Speed:** 20 feet (Walk)

As a Redtail, you have the following Abilities:

### Limited Flight

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | 1 Action     | -              | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| PB/SR    | -            | Innate         | -        |

As an Action you can grant yourself a Fly speed of 30 feet and move using that speed. This Fly speed lasts until the start of your next turn. You can Cast this Ability a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Bird’s Eye View

While you have a Fly speed or an elevated vantage point such as a clifftop or spire, you have Advantage on all Perception checks that involve sight.

### Nomad

You can take a Short Rest while travelling, instead of in a fixed location like a camp. While resting in this way, your rest is interrupted if you perform any strenuous task beyond walking.
