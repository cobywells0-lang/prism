Orcs are a fungal race that tend to value the natural order of things - life, death, decay and new life. They are covered in a carapace of moss, lichen, grass, or other vegetation which they use to hide in plain sight amongst shrubbery, choosing to reveal themselves only when it suits them.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid Plant
**Size:** Small or Medium
**Speed:** 20 feet (Walk)

As an Orc, you have the following Abilities:

### Natural Camouflage

While in a plant-dominated environment, such as a field, garden or forest, you can become Hidden without first being Concealed.

### Fungal Decay

| Level    | Cast Actions | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | Reaction     | Phy/Ins        | 30 feet  |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**  | **Components** | **Area** |
| PB/LR    | -            | Innate         | -        |
As a Reaction when a creature within range dies, your spores can feed upon that creature. You regain a number of Hit Points equal to your Ability bonus and additionally gain that many Temporary Hit Points. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Mycelial Roots

When you would be Pushed or Pulled while not Airborne, you can choose to reduce the distance by 5 feet multiplied by your Proficiency bonus.

Also while not Airborne, you can telepathically communicate with any creature within 500 feet that shares this Ability and is also not Airborne. You can communicate vague concepts and ideas, but not precise information.
