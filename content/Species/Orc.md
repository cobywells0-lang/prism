Orcs are a fungal race that tend to value the natural order of things - life, death, decay and new life. They are covered in a carapace of moss, lichen, grass, or other vegetation, and their movements throw spores into the air around them.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid Plant
**Size:** Small or Medium
**Speed:** 20 feet (Walk)

As an Orc, you have the following species features:

### Spore Aura

As an Action you can shed a cloud of life-giving spores. For up to 1 Minute, when another creature starts its turn within a 10-foot Sphere Emanation, you can choose to heal it for 1d6 Hit Points. The cloud can heal in this way a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus and dissipates early when all of the healing is used. You can use this feature twice, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Fungal Decay

As a Reaction when a creature you can see within 30 feet dies, your spores can feed upon that creature. You regain 1d8 Hit Points and additionally gain that many Temporary Hit Points. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Mycelial Roots

When you would be Pushed or Pulled while not Airborne, you can choose to reduce the distance by 5 feet multiplied by your Proficiency bonus.

Also while not Airborne, you can telepathically communicate with any creature within 500 feet that shares this feature and is also not Airborne. You can communicate vague concepts and ideas, but not precise information.
