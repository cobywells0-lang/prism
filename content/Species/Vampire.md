Vampires are a race of bat-like humanoids, well adapted to the dark, expansive cave systems they call home. Many myths surround these creatures, and those tales are typically not appreciated by the race they vilify.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid Beast
**Size:** Small or Medium  
**Speed:** 20 feet (Walk)

As a Vampire, you have the following Abilities:

### Limited Flight

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats      | Range |
| ----- | ------------ | ---------- | ----- |
| 1     | 1 Action     | -          | Self  |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components** | **Area**  |
| PB/SR | -            | Innate     | -     |

As an Action you can grant yourself a Fly speed of 30 feet and move using that speed. This Fly speed lasts until the start of your next turn. You can Cast this Ability a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Silent Wings

While you are Airborne, you have Advantage on Stealth checks.

### Echolocation

| Level | Cast Actions | Stats      | Range                    |
| ----- | ------------ | ---------- | ------------------------ |
| 1     | 1 Action     | -          | Self                     |
| **Uses**  | **Sustain**      | **Components** | **Area**                     |
| PB/SR | -            | Innate     | Cone Emanation (60-foot) |

As an Action you can produce an almost inaudible sound which you use to navigate. You produce a 60-foot Cone Emanation. This Emanation is blocked by liquids and areas of magical silence in addition to solid surfaces. Creatures and Objects within this Emanation that are Concealed are not Concealed to you. You can Cast this Ability a number of times equal to your Proficiency bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.
