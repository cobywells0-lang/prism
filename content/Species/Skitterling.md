Skitterlings are a race of rodent-like humanoids, sporting sensitive whiskers and long, naked tails. Skitterling culture prizes cunning and resourcefulness above all else. They are tight-knit and easy to forgive amongst their own kind, despite the near-constant one-upping and betrayals. In contrast, they tend to be very secretive and distrusting when in the company of outsiders.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid Beast
**Size:** Small or Medium  
**Speed:** 20 feet (Walk)

As a Skitterling, you have the following Abilities:

### Strong Stomach

You have Advantage on Physique saving throws made to avoid or end the Sickened condition and disease.

### Prehensile Tail

Your tail is capable of holding, equipping, and manipulating items. Whenever you use your tail to make a skill check or saving throw that uses Finesse, you make the roll with Disadvantage. Additionally, when you use your tail to cast an ability that uses Finesse as one of its Stats, any attacks you make as part of that ability are made with Disadvantage.
