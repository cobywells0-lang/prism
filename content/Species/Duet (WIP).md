A Duet is not one individual, but two. Whether they're a mischievous pair of Skitterlings in a trenchcoat, a zombie and spectre working together to be more than the sum of their parts, or a demon-infested child - Duets are inseparable and interdependent.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Small or Medium
**Speed:** 20ft (Walk)

As a Duet, you have the following abilities:

### Duel Form

With two minds, you are more resistant to certain enchantments. You have Advantage on saving throws made to end or avoid the Charmed condition.
