Automata are a mechanical race, the origins of which are uncertain. In place of a head, each Automata has a tome, with each page filled with the ever-shifting runic text that allows it to think. The bodies of Automata are as varied as those of any other race, or even more so.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid Construct
**Size:** Small or Medium
**Speed:** 20 feet (Walk)

As an Automata, you have the following Abilities:

### Mechanical Form

Your body is made of metal, stone, wood, or other processed materials. You do not need to eat, drink or breathe. You are immune to Suffocation.

### Painless

You do not fall [[Unconscious]] when you fall to 0 Hit Points. You instead become [[Prone]] and [[Dazed]] 3, and cannot lose either condition while at 0 Hit Points.

### Self-Customisation

| Level    | Cast Actions      | Stats          | Range    |
| -------- | ----------------- | -------------- | -------- |
| 1        | Activity/1 Action | -              | Self     |
| **Uses** | **Sustain**       | **Components** | **Area** |
| -        | -                 | Weapon/Tool*   | -        |
\**Any Weapon or Tool you have Proficiency with.

As an Activity you can integrate one Weapon or Tool you have Proficiency with into your body, or swap one integrated item for another. You can have no more than one item integrated at a time. As an Action you can retract the item into your body, where it is Hidden, or deploy it such that it can be used freely. You cannot drop or be disarmed of your integrated item while you live.
